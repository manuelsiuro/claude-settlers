# Arrows — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Arrows"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

Arrows are a **processed good** produced at the Fletcher's Workshop from Wood and Iron Bars (4 arrows per batch). They are required alongside a Bow to recruit Archer units at the Archery Range. The arrow bundle should look sharp, precise, and military-ready.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be sharp, precise, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Arrow shafts** — Shaft Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Arrowheads** — Iron Grey (`#7A7A7A`)
- **Fletching** — Red (`#C0392B`)
- **Binding cord** — Tan (`#D2A86E`)

---

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Arrows. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Primary shape:** A bundle of 4 thin, parallel cylinders (Shaft Brown) — each arrow is a long, very thin cylinder. The bundle is tied together at the midpoint.
- **Arrowheads:** Small cone shapes (Iron Grey) at one end of each shaft — the sharp tips pointing upward when the bundle stands.
- **Fletching:** Tiny flat triangular planes (Red) at the opposite end of each shaft — 2–3 per arrow, fanning out slightly. The red fletching provides the most visible color accent.
- **Binding:** A thin torus or band (Tan) wrapped around the middle of the bundle, keeping the 4 arrows together for transport.
- **Scale:** About the length of a serf's arm — a compact bundle. When carried, the serf holds the bundle vertically or slung on their back.
- **Arrangement:** When stacked at a flag, multiple bundles lean against each other — creating a fan-like spread of thin vertical lines with red and grey accents.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Swords (Arrows = thin, bundled parallel cylinders vs Swords = single flat, wide blade). Arrows are thinner and always grouped.
- **Silhouette:** A cluster of thin vertical lines with pointed tips — the only resource that creates this tall, thin, multi-line profile.
- **Stockpile appearance:** Multiple arrow bundles leaning together — looks like a fletcher's workshop display with red fletching visible throughout.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Directional light to catch the grey arrowhead tips and create a subtle metallic glint.
- The red fletching should pop against the brown shafts, making the bundle identifiable at distance.
- At game scale, the thin vertical bundle with red accents should clearly communicate "arrows" from the isometric camera.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
