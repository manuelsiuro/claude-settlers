# Cattle — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Cattle"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

Cattle are **animal resources** raised at the Cattle Ranch. They can be sent to the Slaughterhouse for Meat and raw Leather, making them a key link in the food and crafting chains. The cow should look sturdy, peaceful, and well-fed — a valuable farm animal.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be sturdy, pastoral, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Body** — Cow Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Head/face** — Lighter Brown (`#A0724A`)
- **Horns** — Off-White (`#E0D8C0`)
- **Hooves** — Dark Brown (`#4A3520`)
- **Nose/eyes** — Dark (`#2A2A2A`)

---

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Cattle. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Body:** A large horizontal oblong cuboid or rounded cylinder (Cow Brown) — wider and heavier than the horse. The body is broad and barrel-shaped, conveying the stocky build of a cow.
- **Legs:** Four short, stubby cylinders (Cow Brown) — positioned at the four corners of the body. Shorter and thicker than horse legs. Tiny dark cuboids for hooves (Dark Brown).
- **Head:** A smaller, blocky cuboid (Lighter Brown) extending forward from the body on a thick short neck. The face is blunt and square — not tapered like a horse.
- **Horns:** Two small cone shapes (Off-White) curving slightly outward from the top of the head — the defining cattle feature.
- **Tail:** A thin cylinder (Cow Brown) hanging from the rear, with a tiny tuft (darker cuboid) at the end.
- **Scale:** Larger than pigs but shorter than horses — a medium-large animal. When led to a building, cattle plod along slowly.
- **Arrangement:** When at a Cattle Ranch, 1–2 cattle shapes stand or graze in the fenced pasture area.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Pigs (Cattle = larger, brown, with horns and longer legs vs Pigs = smaller, pink, no horns, very stubby). Horses (Cattle = shorter, stockier, barrel body with horns vs Horses = taller, sleeker, no horns).
- **Silhouette:** A broad, barrel-shaped body on short legs with two horn points on top — the classic cow outline. The horns are the key identifier.
- **Stockpile appearance:** Not stockpiled — cattle are living animals in pastures, not items at flags. They appear in the Cattle Ranch's fenced area.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm pastoral sunlight to bring out the brown tones and give a peaceful, farm-life feeling.
- The off-white horns should catch light and be clearly visible from above — they're the primary distinguishing feature.
- At game scale, the broad brown body with horn points should immediately read as "cow" from the isometric camera.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
