# Wool — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Wool"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

Wool is a **raw material** gathered at the Sheep Farm by the Shepherd. It is processed into Cloth at the Weaver's Hut. Wool should look fluffy, soft, and cloud-like — a natural, unprocessed fiber bundle.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be soft, natural, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Main wool** — Off-White (`#F5F0E0`)
- **Shadow areas** — Warm Grey (`#D8D0C0`)
- **Binding thread** — Tan (`#D2A86E`)

---

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Wool. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Primary shape:** An icosphere (Off-White) with slightly randomized vertices to create a fluffy, cloud-like irregular sphere. The softness and irregularity is key — this should NOT look like a smooth ball. Use a low-subdivision icosphere and push a few vertices outward to break the symmetry.
- **Scale:** About the size of a serf's torso — a generous armful of raw wool. When carried, it sits between the serf's outstretched arms.
- **Binding:** A thin torus or band (Tan) wrapped around the middle of the wool bundle, suggesting it has been loosely tied for transport.
- **Arrangement:** When stacked at a flag, wool bundles sit in a loose pile — 3–5 fluffy spheres heaped together, looking like small clouds on the ground.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Flour sacks (Wool is fluffy and irregular, Flour is a smooth fabric sack with a flat top). The silhouette difference is organic vs geometric.
- **Silhouette:** A soft, bumpy sphere — the only resource with this cloud-like outline. Immediately reads as "fiber" or "fluff."
- **Stockpile appearance:** A pile of wool bundles looks like a heap of small clouds — soft, white, and pillowy. Very distinct from any angular or cylindrical resource.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Soft, diffuse lighting to emphasize the gentle roundness and off-white color — avoid harsh shadows that would make it look solid.
- The off-white wool should glow slightly in sunlight, creating a warm, natural feel.
- At game scale, the fluffy silhouette should be instantly recognizable from the isometric camera as something soft and fibrous.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
