# Cloth — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Cloth"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

Cloth is a **processed good** produced at the Weaver's Hut from Wool. It is used as a crafting material for various purposes. The cloth bolt should look neatly folded and refined — a finished textile product.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be neat, refined, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Main cloth** — Cream (`#F0E8D0`)

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Cloth. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Primary shape:** A folded bolt — a flat, stacked cuboid (Cream) representing fabric folded 3–4 times. The shape is wider than it is tall, with gentle rounded edges suggesting softness rather than rigid geometry.
- **Colored stripe:** A thin band of color (Blue Stripe) running across the top fold — this decorative stripe is the key visual differentiator from other flat/stacked resources.
- **Scale:** About the width of a serf's body — a bolt of cloth carried in both arms. When carried, it rests across the serf's arms horizontally.
- **Arrangement:** When stacked at a flag, cloth bolts pile neatly — flat rectangles stacked with different colored stripes visible (blue, red, green), creating a colorful layered display.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Planks (Cloth = softer-looking flat shape with colored stripe vs Planks = longer, thinner wooden rectangles with wood brown color). The cream color and decorative stripe immediately say "fabric."
- **Silhouette:** A wide, flat, stacked rectangle — similar to planks but shorter and wider, with softer proportions.
- **Stockpile appearance:** A neat stack of folded cloth bolts with colorful stripes visible — looks like a textile shop display. The variety of stripe colors adds visual interest.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Soft, even lighting to bring out the cream color and make the colored stripe pop with contrast.
- The cloth should appear soft and light — avoid harsh shadows that would make it look heavy or rigid.
- At game scale, the colored stripe across the cream surface should be the identifying feature from the isometric camera.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
