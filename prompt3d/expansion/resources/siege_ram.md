# Siege Ram — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Siege Ram"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

The Siege Ram is a **processed good** produced at the Siege Workshop from 3 Wood and 2 Iron Bars. It is required to recruit Siege Operator military units. The siege ram should look massive, heavy, and imposing — a weapon of war designed to breach fortifications.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be massive, imposing, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Main log** — Dark Wood (`#5C3A1E`)
- **Iron head** — Iron Grey (`#4A4A4A`)
- **Iron bands** — Band Grey (`#5A5A5A`)
- **Handle wraps** — Rope Tan (`#D2A86E`)

---

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Siege Ram. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Primary shape:** A large, long cylinder (Dark Wood) — the main battering ram log. This is the largest single resource in the game, about 3× the length of a normal resource item.
- **Iron head:** A blunt cone shape (Iron Grey) at one end of the log — the reinforced battering tip that smashes through gates and walls. Slightly wider than the log diameter.
- **Iron bands:** 2–3 thin torus rings (Band Grey) spaced along the log — reinforcement bands preventing the wood from splitting under impact.
- **Handle positions:** Small cylindrical pegs (Dark Wood) protruding from the sides at regular intervals — where siege operators grip and swing the ram.
- **Scale:** The largest resource item — about the length of 2 serfs laid end to end. When transported, it requires a siege operator to carry and is highly visible.
- **Arrangement:** When at a flag, siege rams lay horizontally on the ground — their massive length makes them the most imposing stockpile item.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Wood logs (Siege Ram = much larger with iron tip and reinforcement bands vs Wood logs = smaller, plain, and uniform). The iron head and bands are the key distinguishers.
- **Silhouette:** A long horizontal cylinder with a conical tip — the longest, most dramatic resource silhouette. Unmistakable.
- **Stockpile appearance:** One or two massive logs with iron fittings laying on the ground — even a single siege ram dominates the visual space around a flag.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Strong directional light to create contrast between the dark wood and the metallic grey iron fittings.
- The iron head should have a slightly brighter reflection to suggest hardened metal — the business end of a weapon of war.
- At game scale, the sheer size of the siege ram should communicate "heavy military equipment" immediately from the isometric camera.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
