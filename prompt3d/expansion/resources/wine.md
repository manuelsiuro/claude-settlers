# Wine — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Wine"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

Wine is a **processed good** produced at the Winery from Grapes. It serves as both food (0.30 satiation) and drink for morale at the Inn/Tavern. The wine bottle should look refined and elegant — a prized luxury good.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be refined, elegant, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Bottle body** — Deep Red (`#8B1A1A`)
- **Bottle glass** — Dark Glass (`#3A1A1A`)
- **Cork** — Cork Tan (`#D2A86E`)
- **Label suggestion** — Cream (`#F0E8D0`)

---

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Wine. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Primary shape:** A bottle form — a cylinder body (Deep Red) tapering into a cone neck that narrows to a small flat top. The body is about 2/3 of the total height, the neck is the upper 1/3. A tiny cylinder or cube (Cork Tan) sits on top as the cork.
- **Scale:** Slightly taller than it is wide — about the height of a serf's torso. When carried, the serf holds it upright in one hand.
- **Label (optional):** A tiny flat rectangle (Cream) on the front of the bottle body — just a color suggestion, not readable text.
- **Arrangement:** When stacked at a flag, wine bottles stand upright in rows — 3–5 bottles side by side create a distinctive row of tall, narrow shapes.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Beer (Wine = tall, narrow bottle shape vs Beer = short, wide mug/keg). The deep red color is also unique — no other resource shares this dark wine-red.
- **Silhouette:** A tall, narrow tapered shape — the classic bottle profile. Immediately reads as "bottle" from any angle.
- **Stockpile appearance:** A row or cluster of dark red bottles standing upright — elegant and ordered, like a wine cellar display.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- The deep red should appear rich and luxurious in warm directional light — this is a prestige resource.
- A subtle highlight on the bottle shoulder to suggest glass-like quality without actual reflections.
- At game scale, the tall bottle silhouette and deep red color should be unmistakable from the isometric camera.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
