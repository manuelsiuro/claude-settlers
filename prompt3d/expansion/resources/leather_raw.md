# Leather (Raw) — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Leather (Raw)"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

Raw Leather is a **raw material** — a byproduct from the Cattle Ranch and Slaughterhouse. It is processed into worked leather at the Tannery. It should look like an unprocessed animal hide — flat, rough-edged, and organic.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be rugged, natural, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Main hide** — Tan (`#C4A060`)
- **Darker edges** — Dark Tan (`#A08040`)
- **Underside hint** — Light Tan (`#D8C088`)

---

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Raw Leather. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Primary shape:** A flat, irregular plane (Tan) — like a flattened, slightly wavy animal hide. Start with a flat plane and irregular-scale the edges to create an organic, non-rectangular outline. The shape should suggest a stretched-out animal skin.
- **Scale:** About as wide as a serf's body — a single hide draped or folded for carrying. When carried, it hangs over one arm.
- **Surface detail:** A slight wave or buckle in the flat surface — not perfectly flat, but with subtle undulation suggesting the stiffness of untanned rawhide.
- **Arrangement:** When stacked at a flag, raw hides drape over each other in a loose pile — overlapping flat shapes with edges visible, like a stack of leather sheets.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Worked Leather (raw = flat, rough, irregular edges vs worked = smooth, rolled cylinder). The raw version is clearly unfinished.
- **Silhouette:** A flat, irregular outline — the only resource that looks like a draped flat shape. No other resource has this organic, flat profile.
- **Stockpile appearance:** A stack of overlapping flat hides — like layered pancakes with irregular edges. Looks raw and unprocessed.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm directional light to bring out the tan tones and show the slight surface undulation through shadow.
- The flat, irregular shape should cast interesting shadows, reinforcing the organic feel.
- At game scale, the tan color and flat profile should clearly communicate "animal hide" from the isometric camera.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
