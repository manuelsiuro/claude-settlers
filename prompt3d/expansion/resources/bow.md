# Bow — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Bow"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

The Bow is a **processed good** produced at the Fletcher's Workshop. Together with Arrows, it is required to recruit Archer units at the Archery Range. The bow should look elegant and functional — a precision weapon crafted from wood.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be elegant, functional, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Bow wood** — Warm Brown (`#A0724A`)
- **Bowstring** — Tan (`#D2A86E`)
- **Grip wrap** — Dark Brown (`#5C3A1E`)

---

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Bow. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Primary shape:** A curved arc — a thin, flat, curved shape (Warm Brown) forming the classic bow curve. Model as a torus segment (about 120–150 degrees of arc) or bend a thin cuboid into a gentle C-curve. The curve should be graceful and symmetrical.
- **Bowstring:** A thin straight line (Tan) — a very thin cylinder connecting the two tips of the bow arc. The string creates the taut, ready-to-fire tension.
- **Grip:** A slightly thicker section at the center of the bow (Dark Brown) — a thin cylinder or cuboid wrapped around the midpoint where the archer holds it.
- **Scale:** About the height of a serf's torso — a shortbow suitable for the game's low-poly proportions. When carried, the serf holds it at their side or slung on their back.
- **Arrangement:** When stacked at a flag, bows lean against each other or hang from a rack — curved shapes creating elegant overlapping arcs.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Scythe (Bow = smaller curved shape with a taut string connecting the tips vs Scythe = long straight handle with blade at one end). The string is the key distinguishing element.
- **Silhouette:** A curved arc with a straight line across — the classic bow shape is instantly recognizable from any angle.
- **Stockpile appearance:** Multiple bows leaning together — curved arcs with strings visible, looking like a weapon rack.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm directional light to highlight the wood grain tones of the curved limb.
- The tan bowstring should be visible against the bow, catching light as a straight accent across the curve.
- At game scale, the distinctive arc-and-string silhouette should immediately read as a bow from the isometric camera.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
