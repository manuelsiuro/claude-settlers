# Horses — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Horse"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

Horses are **animal resources** bred at the Stable from Grain and Hay. They are used either for Cavalry recruitment (Horse + Sword + Shield at a military building) or assigned as transport animals pulling carts on the road network. The horse should look strong, graceful, and noble — a prized military and economic asset.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be noble, powerful, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Body** — Chestnut (`#A0522D`)
- **Mane and tail** — Dark Brown (`#5C3A1E`)
- **Hooves** — Dark Brown (`#4A3520`)
- **Eyes** — Black (`#2A2A2A`)
- **Nose/muzzle** — Light Brown (`#C4A56E`)

---

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Horse. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Body:** A horizontal oblong cylinder or rounded cuboid (Chestnut) — longer and sleeker than cattle. The body is about 2× as long as it is tall, with a graceful, athletic build.
- **Legs:** Four tall, slender cylinders (Chestnut) — longer and thinner than cattle legs, built for speed. Tiny dark cuboids for hooves (Dark Brown).
- **Head:** A tapered cylinder or cone (Chestnut) on a longer, more elegant neck than cattle. Two small triangular ears on top. The face tapers to a softer muzzle (Light Brown).
- **Mane:** A ridge or series of thin flat planes (Dark Brown) running along the top of the neck from ears to shoulders — the flowing mane distinguishes horses from all other animals.
- **Tail:** A longer, flowing cylinder or tapered shape (Dark Brown) extending from the rear — more dramatic than the cattle's thin tail.
- **Scale:** Taller and sleeker than cattle, the tallest animal in the game. When at the Stable, horses stand in stalls.
- **Arrangement:** Horses appear in the Stable's stalls or are led along roads — not stockpiled at flags like regular resources.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Donkeys (Horses = taller, sleeker, chestnut brown, flowing mane and tail vs Donkeys = shorter, stockier, grey-brown, large ears, stubby tail). Cattle (Horses = taller, no horns, elegant build vs Cattle = shorter, barrel body, horns).
- **Silhouette:** A tall, elegant body on long legs with a flowing mane ridge and tail — the classic horse profile. Taller and more graceful than any other animal.
- **Stockpile appearance:** Not stockpiled — horses are living animals in stables or on roads, not items at flags.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm directional light to bring out the rich chestnut coat — the horse should look powerful and well-groomed.
- The dark mane and tail should contrast against the lighter body, emphasizing the flowing lines.
- At game scale, the tall, sleek silhouette with flowing mane should immediately read as "horse" from the isometric camera — clearly nobler than the stocky donkey or cattle.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
