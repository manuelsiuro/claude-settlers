# Water Barrel — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Water Barrel"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

The Water Barrel is a small barrel of fresh water produced at the Well. It serves as a key input for the Brewery, where it is combined with Grain to produce Beer. Water is one of the fundamental resources in the beverage production chain.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be sturdy, functional, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Barrel staves** — Barrel Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Iron bands** — Iron Grey (`#4A4A4A`)
- **Water tint on lid** — Water Blue (`#5599CC`)
- **Lid wood** — Light Brown (`#A0724A`)
- **Barrel shadow/groove** — Dark Brown (`#5C3A1E`)

---

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Water Barrel. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Primary shape:** A small cylinder (8-10 sides for a faceted low-poly look) forming the barrel body. Give it a very slight outward bulge at the middle — select the central edge loop and scale it outward by about 5-10% to suggest the classic barrel curvature. The top and bottom faces should be flat.
- **Iron bands:** Two thin torus rings or flattened cylinders (Iron Grey) wrapped around the barrel — one near the top and one near the bottom, each inset about 15% from the ends. These bands are the key detail that says "barrel" rather than just "cylinder."
- **Water marking:** The top face (lid) should be a separate flat cylinder colored Water Blue or have a blue-tinted disc sitting flush on top. This blue lid is the critical visual cue that distinguishes the Water Barrel from any other barrel in the game.
- **Scale:** Small enough for a serf to carry under one arm — about 60% of a serf's torso height. Wider than it is tall, giving it a squat, sturdy proportions.
- **Arrangement:** When stockpiled, 3-4 barrels stand upright in a tight cluster, their blue lids clearly visible from the isometric top-down angle. Some may be tilted slightly against each other.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Other barrel-shaped items or generic storage. The blue-tinted lid is the signature element — no other resource in the game uses this combination of barrel shape plus blue marking. Without the blue, it would just look like a generic wooden container.
- **Silhouette:** From the isometric camera, the barrel reads as a squat cylinder with visible band lines. The blue top face is clearly visible from the elevated camera angle, making identification instant.
- **Stockpile appearance:** A group of 3-4 water barrels standing together looks like a small supply station — neat rows of brown barrels with blue tops catching the eye. The regularity of the barrel shape makes for a tidy, organized stockpile.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm directional light from above to illuminate the blue lid prominently — since the isometric camera looks down at an angle, the top face is one of the most visible surfaces.
- The Iron Grey bands should catch a slight highlight on their upper edges, adding definition to the barrel's silhouette and breaking up the brown surface.
- At game scale, the blue top is the primary read. Ensure the Water Blue is saturated enough to stand out against the brown barrel body and the earth-toned ground beneath.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
