# Cheese — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Cheese"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

Cheese is a **processed good** produced at the Cheese Maker from Milk. It serves as a mid-tier food source (0.55 satiation). The cheese wedge should look appetizing and artisanal — a wholesome dairy product.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be warm, appetizing, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Cheese body** — Pale Yellow (`#F0D850`)
- **Rind** — Darker Yellow (`#D4B830`)
- **Cut face** — Light Yellow (`#F8E870`)
- **Wax coating (optional)** — Wax Red (`#C0392B`)

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Cheese. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Primary shape:** A wedge — a triangular prism (Pale Yellow). Create a cube, then cut it diagonally to form a wedge shape. The cut face should be slightly lighter (Light Yellow) to show where it was sliced from a larger wheel.
- **Alternative shape:** A short, fat cylinder (Pale Yellow) representing a small cheese wheel — either form works; the wedge is more distinctive.
- **Rind:** The outer curved surface (if wheel) or the two flat triangular sides (if wedge) are slightly darker (Darker Yellow) — suggesting a natural rind.
- **Scale:** About the size of a serf's hand — a single serving wedge or small personal wheel. When carried, the serf holds it in one hand.
- **Arrangement:** When stacked at a flag, cheese wedges or wheels cluster together — wedges pointing different directions, or wheels stacked 2–3 high.

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Gold Bars (Cheese = wedge/wheel shape with organic yellow vs Gold = rectangular ingot with metallic bright yellow). The cheese yellow is warmer and softer than gold's bright gleam.
- **Silhouette:** A triangular wedge or short fat disc — unique among all resources. No other resource has this distinctive cheese-wedge profile.
- **Stockpile appearance:** A collection of yellow wedges and small wheels — immediately reads as a cheese shop display.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm directional light to bring out the appetizing pale yellow color — cheese should look wholesome and inviting.
- The lighter cut face of the wedge should catch more light, showing the contrast between rind and interior.
- At game scale, the yellow wedge shape should be clearly distinct from gold bars — rounder edges, warmer tone, organic feel.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
