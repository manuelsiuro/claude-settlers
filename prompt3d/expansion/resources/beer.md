# Beer — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Beer"** resource in a low-poly 3D style.

Beer is a **processed good** produced at the Brewery from Grain and a Water Barrel. It serves as both food (0.25 satiation) and drink for morale at the Inn/Tavern. The beer mug should look hearty and rustic — a staple tavern drink.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the resource model. The final look should be hearty, rustic, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Beer liquid** — Amber (`#DAA520`)
- **Mug wood** — Mug Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Foam** — White (`#F0F0F0`)
- **Mug bands** — Iron Grey (`#5A5A5A`)

---

## 2. SHAPE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Beer. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **Primary shape:** A wooden mug — a short, wide cylinder (Mug Brown) with a flat bottom and open top. Slightly wider at the top than the bottom. A small handle on one side — a half-torus or curved cuboid (Mug Brown) attached to the side.
- **Foam cap:** A slightly bulging disc or short cylinder (White) sitting on top of the mug, slightly overflowing the rim — the frothy head of beer.
- **Iron bands:** 2 thin torus rings (Iron Grey) wrapped around the mug body — top and bottom — like the bands on a wooden barrel.
- **Scale:** About the size of a serf's head — a hearty drinking vessel. When carried, the serf holds it by the handle in one hand.
- **Arrangement:** When stacked at a flag, mugs cluster together — 3–5 mugs in a group, some slightly tilted, giving a casual tavern feel.

---

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY & CONTEXT

Describe what makes this resource visually distinct:

- **Distinguish from:** Wine (Beer = short, wide mug with foam vs Wine = tall, narrow bottle). The amber color and white foam cap are unique to beer.
- **Silhouette:** A short, wide cylinder with a handle bump on one side and foam on top — the classic mug shape. Very different from the tall wine bottle.
- **Stockpile appearance:** A cluster of wooden mugs — some with visible foam tops. Looks like a tavern counter.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm amber lighting to make the beer color glow — evoking a cozy tavern atmosphere.
- The white foam should catch light and stand out against the brown mug and amber liquid.
- At game scale, the mug handle and foam cap should be visible from the isometric camera, confirming this is beer and not a barrel.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
