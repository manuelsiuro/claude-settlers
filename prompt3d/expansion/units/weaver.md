# Weaver — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Weaver"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Weaver is a **resource processing serf** — working at the Weaver's Hut to transform raw Wool into finished Cloth. This character is a textile artisan, immediately recognizable by the colorful band of threads wrapped around the waist and the small weaving shuttle held in one hand. The Weaver should look creative, nimble, and industrious.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be colorful, crafty, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Thread Accent Blue** — Blue (`#4488BB`)

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Weaver character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5x as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60-70% of the body width.
- **The headscarf:** A small, slightly flattened half-sphere or rounded cuboid (Cream) draped over the top-back of the head. It should sit snugly on top of the skull and extend slightly past the back — a simple textile worker's cap that hints at the profession without being too elaborate.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends forward holding the shuttle. The other arm rests at the side or is tucked close to the body. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Weaver's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The colorful thread band (primary identifier):** A horizontal ring of three narrow, stacked flat tori or thin cylinders wrapped around the waist of the body — one Blue (`#4488BB`), one Red (`#CC3333`), one Gold (`#DAA520`). Each ring should be thin (about 10% of body height) and sit flush against the body surface. This tri-color band is unique among all serfs and provides an unmistakable splash of color visible from any camera angle. Think of it as a belt made of vibrant yarn.
- **The weaving shuttle (secondary identifier):** A small, flat, pointed cuboid (Warm Brown `#A0724A`) held in one hand — shaped like a miniature boat or elongated diamond. Start with a cube, scale it flat on one axis, then taper both ends to points by scaling down the end faces. The shuttle should be about 40% of the body width in length. A tiny thread accent (Blue or Gold) can trail from one end of the shuttle for extra charm.
- **Cloth draped over shoulder (optional accent):** A small flat cuboid (Cream `#F0E8D0`) draped over one shoulder, representing a piece of finished cloth. This reinforces the textile-worker identity and adds a soft, flowing visual element.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and illuminate the colorful thread band around the waist.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character and let all three thread colors remain distinct.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The tri-color thread band should be the eye-catching feature from the isometric game camera angle — blue, red, and gold stripes popping against the light brown body.
- The warm brown shuttle and cream cloth accent provide secondary reads that reinforce the textile-crafting role.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
