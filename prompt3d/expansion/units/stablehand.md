# Stablehand — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Stablehand"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Stablehand is a **resource gathering serf** — working at the Stable to raise and tend Horses and Donkeys. This is a practical, hands-on worker who spends the day mucking stalls and forking hay, identified by a brown leather vest and a distinctive pitchfork. The Stablehand should look sturdy, down-to-earth, and reliable.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be practical, earthy, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Stablehand character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5x as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60-70% of the body width.
- **No hat:** The Stablehand goes bareheaded — the leather vest and pitchfork provide all the visual identity needed. This also differentiates the Stablehand from the hat-wearing Farmer and Rancher.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to hold the pitchfork upright. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Stablehand's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The leather vest (co-primary identifier):** A slightly wider, flat cuboid (Warm Brown `#8B5E3C`) layered over the front and back of the body, extending from the shoulders to the waist. The vest should be slightly darker than the base Light Brown clothing beneath it, creating a visible two-tone layered look. Leave the sides open — the vest is like a tabard or sleeveless jerkin. This gives the Stablehand a rugged, working-outdoors appearance.
- **The pitchfork (co-primary identifier):** A long, thin cylinder (Warm Brown `#A0724A`) as the handle, extending taller than the character — about 120% of character height. At the top, attach 3 small thin cylinders or elongated cones (Steel Grey `#7A7A7A`) pointing upward in a fan arrangement — these are the prongs. Each prong should be about 20% of the handle length. The three-pronged fork silhouette is instantly recognizable and unique among all serf tools.
- **Hay gold accents (supporting detail):** 2-3 tiny flat rectangles or thin cuboids (Hay Gold `#D4B84A`) placed on the vest or body surface — suggesting bits of straw and hay clinging to the clothing. These small golden flecks add storytelling detail and a warm accent color. Place them at different angles for a natural, scattered look.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm, golden directional sunlight from above-left — evoking a stable yard atmosphere and making the hay gold accents glow warmly.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character and keep the vest layering visible.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The brown vest over light brown clothing should create a clear two-tone silhouette from the isometric game camera angle, while the tall pitchfork with its three prongs adds a distinctive top-heavy profile.
- The hay gold flecks provide cheerful warm accents that tell the story of the profession without needing any additional props.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
