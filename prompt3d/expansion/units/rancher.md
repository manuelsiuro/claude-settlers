# Rancher — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Rancher"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Rancher is a **resource gathering serf** — working at the Cattle Ranch to herd and manage Cattle across the settlement's pastures. This rugged outdoorsman is distinguished by a wide-brimmed hat and a lasso coil, giving the character a distinctly frontier-rancher personality. The Rancher should look tough, weather-beaten, and self-reliant.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be rugged, frontier-inspired, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60-70% of the body width.

The brim extends about 40-50% past the head on all sides. Add a slightly taller crown cylinder on top of the brim to create a more structured hat shape — taller and more defined than the Farmer's flatter sun hat.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm holds or has the lasso coil slung over the shoulder. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).
