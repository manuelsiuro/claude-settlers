# Vintner — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Vintner"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Vintner is a **resource gathering serf** — working at the Vineyard to tend vines and harvest Grapes. This character spends the day among the rows of grapevines, pruning and picking, and should look practical and outdoorsy with a distinctive purple-stained apron that immediately signals grape work.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be practical, vine-stained, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends forward holding the pruning shears. The other arm rests at the side. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).
