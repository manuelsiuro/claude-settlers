# Engineer — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Engineer"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Engineer is a **specialized civilian serf** — working at the Siege Workshop to design and build Siege Rams and other war machines. Unlike most laborers, this character has an intellectual, craftsman quality — identified by a leather cap, tool belt, and a rolled blueprint scroll. The Engineer should look clever, capable, and methodical.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be inventive, meticulous, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Engineer character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5x as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60-70% of the body width.
- **The leather cap:** A flat, rounded cylinder (Warm Brown `#8B5E3C`) placed on the top of the head — like a beret or skullcap. It should sit snugly, covering the top hemisphere of the head without extending past the sides. Scale the top face down slightly to give it a gentle dome shape. This is a working cap, not a fashionable hat.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends forward holding the blueprint scroll. The other arm rests at the side near the tool belt. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Engineer's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The leather cap (co-primary identifier):** A flat, rounded cylinder (Warm Brown `#8B5E3C`) sitting on the top of the head. Use a cylinder with about 4-6 segments, scale the top face inward slightly to create a gentle dome. The cap should cover only the top of the skull — about 30% of the head height. This practical headwear immediately distinguishes the Engineer from bare-headed serfs and from the wider hats of Farmers and Ranchers.
- **The tool belt (co-primary identifier):** A thin torus or flat ring (Dark Brown `#5C3A1E`) around the waist. Attach 2-3 tiny cuboids or cylinders (Steel Grey `#7A7A7A`) hanging from the belt at different angles — representing a small hammer, wrench, and pliers. Each tool is tiny (about 15% of body width) but their presence is what makes the Engineer look like a builder rather than a laborer. The dark brown belt provides strong contrast against the light brown body.
- **The blueprint scroll (secondary identifier):** A small cylinder (Cream `#F0E8D0`) held in one hand, about 30% of body height in length. Add a thin flat rectangle partially unrolled from one end, tinted with Blueprint Blue (`#5599CC`) to suggest technical drawings. The blue accent is unique to the Engineer and signals the intellectual nature of the profession.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and highlight the leather cap and tool belt details.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character and keep the tiny tool shapes readable.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The leather cap, dangling tools, and blue-tinted scroll should create a layered, detailed silhouette from the isometric game camera angle — the Engineer looks more accessorized than most serfs.
- The Blueprint Blue accent on the scroll provides a cool color note that is unique among the warm-toned serf roster, immediately communicating the design-and-planning role.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
