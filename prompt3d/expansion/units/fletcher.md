# Fletcher — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Fletcher"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Fletcher is a **military-support processing serf** — working at the Fletcher's Workshop to craft Arrows and Bows from Wood and other materials. This character bridges the civilian and military worlds, wearing a distinctive forest green tunic and carrying a bundle of finished arrows. The Fletcher should look sharp, focused, and ranger-like.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be sharp, woodland-inspired, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Body / Tunic** — Forest Green (`#4A7A3F`)
- **Head / Skin** — Light Peach (`#F5D5B8`)
- **Arrow Shafts** — Tan (`#D2A86E`)
- **Arrowheads** — Steel Grey (`#7A7A7A`)
- **Belt** — Warm Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Fletching Feathers** — Red (`#C0392B`)

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Fletcher character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid — using Forest Green (`#4A7A3F`) instead of the usual Light Brown. This green tunic immediately signals the Fletcher's connection to military crafting and the woodland materials used in arrow-making. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5x as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60-70% of the body width.
- **No hat:** The Fletcher has no special headwear — the green tunic color and arrow bundle provide all the visual identity needed. The bare head also differentiates the Fletcher from the Forester (who wears a green hat).
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm cradles the arrow bundle against the body or holds it to the side. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Forest Green, matching the tunic).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Fletcher's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The forest green tunic (co-primary identifier):** The entire body is Forest Green (`#4A7A3F`) — a rich, earthy green that ties the Fletcher to both the woodland source materials and the military output. This color is distinct from the Forester's brighter green hat and creates a ranger-like appearance. The green body color makes the Fletcher one of only a few serfs with non-brown clothing.
- **The arrow bundle (co-primary identifier):** A cluster of 3-4 thin, parallel cylinders (Tan `#D2A86E`) held together, each about 70% of the character's height. At the top of each shaft, add a tiny cone (Steel Grey `#7A7A7A`) as the arrowhead. At the bottom, add very small flat triangles or tiny wedge shapes (Red `#C0392B`) as fletching feathers. The arrows should be grouped tightly and held in one hand or tucked under an arm. The parallel lines of the arrow shafts create a distinctive silhouette.
- **The leather belt (supporting detail):** A thin torus or flat ring (Warm Brown `#8B5E3C`) around the waist of the body. Optionally, attach a small flat cuboid pouch to the belt — suggesting the Fletcher carries small tools or spare arrowheads. The belt breaks up the solid green of the tunic.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and bring out the rich green of the tunic.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character and keep the arrow details readable.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The forest green body should stand out clearly from the isometric camera angle as one of the few non-brown serfs — the color itself communicates the military-adjacent role.
- The tan arrow shafts with grey tips and red fletching provide fine detail that rewards a closer look, while the overall green+tan color combination reads clearly even at distance.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
