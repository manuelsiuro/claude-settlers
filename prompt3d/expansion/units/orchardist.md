Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Orchardist"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be cheerful, pastoral, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Orchardist character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5x as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The hat:** A wide, flat cylinder (Light Tan) placed on top of the head — similar in shape to the Farmer's hat but noticeably lighter in color. The brim should extend about 40% past the head on all sides. Add a very short cylinder on top as the crown. The lighter tan immediately distinguishes this from the Farmer's darker brown hat.
- **The limbs (simplified):** Both arms extend forward and slightly downward to cradle the fruit basket. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).
