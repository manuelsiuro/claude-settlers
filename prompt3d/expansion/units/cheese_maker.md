# Cheese Maker — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Cheese Maker"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Cheese Maker is a **resource processing serf** — working at the Cheese Maker building to process Milk into Cheese. This character is defined by a warm yellow color scheme — a pale yellow apron paired with a small carried cheese wheel — making them one of the most colorfully distinctive serfs in the game.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be warm, artisanal, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Cheese Maker character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5x as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The white cap:** A short, flat cylinder (White `#F0F0F0`) placed on top of the head. This is a simple hygiene cap — much shorter and flatter than the Baker's tall chef hat, only about 20–25% of the head's height. Think of a small pillbox shape that sits on the crown. It signals "food processing" without mimicking the Baker's signature tall toque.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends forward at chest height, holding the cheese wheel out for display. The other arm supports from below or rests at the side. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Cheese Maker's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The pale yellow apron (primary identifier):** A flat cuboid (Pale Yellow `#F0D850`) attached to the front of the body, extending from chest to below the waist. Slightly narrower than the body. The flat disc shape with its squat proportions unmistakably reads as a cheese wheel. Optionally, add a thin ring of red (Red `#C0392B`) around the edge to suggest a wax rind coating — this red accent provides a striking color pop against the yellow.
- **The white cap (tertiary detail):** The short white cap distinguishes the Cheese Maker's head from the Baker's tall hat, the Dairymaid's bonnet, and bareheaded serfs. Its compact shape keeps the overall silhouette focused on the yellow apron and cheese wheel below.
