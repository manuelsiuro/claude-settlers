# Brewer — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Brewer"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Brewer is a **resource processing serf** — working at the Brewery to combine Grain and Water into Beer. This character should look sturdy and convivial, with an amber-colored apron and a frothy wooden mug that instantly evoke the craft of ale-making.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be sturdy, convivial, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Brewer character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5x as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel. The Brewer can be modeled very slightly wider than the standard serf to suggest a robust, barrel-chested build.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The soft cap:** A flattened half-sphere or very short cylinder (Tan `#D2A86E`) placed snugly on top of the head. This is a soft, slouchy cap — lower and rounder than the Vintner's workman's cap, evoking a medieval tavern keeper. It adds minimal height to the silhouette.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends forward holding the wooden mug at chest height. The other arm rests at the side. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Brewer's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The amber apron (primary identifier):** A flat cuboid (Amber `#DAA520`) attached to the front of the body, extending from chest to below the waist. Slightly narrower than the body. The warm golden-amber color is unique among all serfs — distinct from the Butcher's maroon, the Vintner's purple, and the Baker's white. It immediately evokes grain, barley, and ale. This golden hue is the Brewer's most recognizable feature.
- **The wooden mug with foam (co-primary identifier):** A small open-topped cylinder (Brown `#8B5E3C`) held in one hand, representing a classic tankard. On top, place a tiny flattened half-sphere or disc (White `#F0F0F0`) representing a cap of frothy foam overflowing the rim. Optionally add a small cuboid handle on one side of the mug. The white foam cap on the brown mug is a delightful detail that reads clearly even at small sizes — it is the visual shorthand for "beer."
- **The soft cap (tertiary detail):** The low, round tan cap distinguishes the Brewer's head from bareheaded serfs and from the taller or wider hats of other professions. It suggests a casual indoor worker.

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:
