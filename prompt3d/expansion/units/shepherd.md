# Shepherd — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Shepherd"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Shepherd is a **resource gathering serf** — working at the Sheep Farm to tend flocks and gather Wool. This gentle, patient figure wears a distinctive cream-colored wool cloak and carries a tall shepherd's crook, creating a unique draped silhouette that is taller and softer than any other serf. The Shepherd should look calm, protective, and pastoral.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be gentle, pastoral, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Wool Cloak** — Cream (`#F0E8D0`)
- **Crook Staff** — Warm Brown (`#A0724A`)

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Shepherd character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing underneath the cloak. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5x as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60-70% of the body width.
- **The hood:** A small half-sphere or rounded cuboid (Warm Brown `#8B5E3C`) attached to the back of the head, resting behind and slightly above the skull. The hood is down (not covering the face) but visible from behind and the sides — it suggests the Shepherd can pull it up in bad weather.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to hold the crook upright. The other arm is mostly hidden beneath the cloak. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Shepherd's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The wool cloak/poncho (primary identifier):** A larger cone or slightly flared cylinder (Cream `#F0E8D0`) draped over the body — wider at the bottom than at the shoulders. This cloak should extend from the shoulders to below the waist, approximately 80% of body height. Make it about 120-130% of the body width at the base, tapering to 90% at the top. The cream color represents wool fabric and is the Shepherd's most unique feature — no other serf has this draped, flowing outer garment. The cloak completely changes the character's silhouette from the standard cylindrical serf shape to a broader, cone-like form.
- **The shepherd's crook (co-primary identifier):** A tall, thin cylinder (Warm Brown `#A0724A`) as the staff, extending about 130% of the character's height — making this the tallest accessory of any serf. At the top, the staff curves into a hook shape: use a half-torus or a bent cylinder curving forward and down. The hook portion should be about 20% of the total staff length. The tall crook combined with the wide cloak creates an unmistakable tall, draped silhouette visible from anywhere on the map.
- **Wool texture accents (supporting detail):** 2-3 small spheres or icospheres (Wool White `#F5F0E0`) placed along the bottom edge of the cloak or on the shoulders — suggesting tufts of wool clinging to the fabric. Each tuft is tiny (about 10% of body width) but the soft, rounded shapes reinforce the sheep-tending profession. The slightly warm white differs from pure white, giving an organic wool-like quality.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm, soft directional sunlight from above-left to gently illuminate the cream cloak — the large, light-colored surface should be the brightest element on the character.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows and maintain the gentle, pastoral mood of the character.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The cream cloak and tall crook should create the most distinctive silhouette of any serf from the isometric game camera angle — a wide, draped base with a tall curved staff rising well above the head. No other serf has this combination.
- The warm brown hood and crook provide grounding earth-tone accents against the light cream, preventing the character from looking washed out.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
