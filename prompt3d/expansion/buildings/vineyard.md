# Vineyard — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Vineyard"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Vineyard is a **resource gathering building** (Tier 2) — it employs a Vintner who cultivates grapevines and harvests Grapes. The Vineyard should look like a small Mediterranean-style shed flanked by neat rows of trellised vines heavy with purple grape clusters — a sunlit, orderly scene of viticulture.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be Mediterranean, sunlit, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Grape clusters** — Grape Purple (`#6B2D8B`)
- **Vine leaves and growth** — Vine Green (`#4A8C3F`)
- **Shed walls and door** — Warm Brown (`#A0724A`)
- **Trellis posts and rails** — Trellis Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Ground and pathways** — Earth Tan (`#C4A060`)
- **Shed foundation and accents** — Light Stone (`#D0C8B0`)

---

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Vineyard. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The shed:** A small stone-and-wood shed (Light Stone lower walls with Warm Brown timber upper half, Trellis Brown pitched roof). Keep this compact — it is a tool and storage hut, not the main visual focus. Add a simple door on the front face and one small window on the side.
- **The trellis rows:** 3–4 parallel rows of wooden trellis structures extending outward from the shed. Each trellis is made of vertical cuboid posts (Trellis Brown) connected by thin horizontal rails at two heights. Between and along the rails, add irregular clusters of flat cuboid or slightly rounded shapes (Vine Green) to represent leafy vine growth. Intersperse small grouped spheres (Grape Purple) hanging below the upper rail — these are the grape clusters, the building's signature visual element.
- **The ground plane:** A flat area of Earth Tan between the trellis rows, suggesting dry, sun-baked soil ideal for grape growing. Add a narrow stone pathway (Light Stone flat cuboids) running between the rows from the shed to the far end.
- **Row spacing:** Leave clear walkable gaps between each trellis row so the Vintner can tend the vines — this orderly spacing is key to the vineyard aesthetic.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key vineyard elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Grape baskets:** 2–3 wide, shallow baskets (Trellis Brown tapered cuboids or cylinders) near the shed entrance, overflowing with tiny purple sphere clusters — freshly picked grapes ready for collection.
- **Pruning tools:** A pair of pruning shears and a small curved knife (thin flat cuboids in a steel grey) leaning against the shed wall or hanging from a hook — the Vintner's essential implements.
- **Wooden stakes:** Several extra trellis stakes (thin vertical cylinders in Trellis Brown) bundled together and leaning against the side of the shed, ready to extend or repair the rows.
- **Stone pathway:** A series of flat, slightly irregular cuboids (Light Stone) forming a path from the shed door into the vine rows. Offset them slightly for a natural, hand-laid look.
- **A water jug:** A small cylinder with a tapered top (Earth Tan) sitting near the shed entrance — the Vintner stays hydrated under the sun.
- **Fallen leaves:** A few tiny flat diamond shapes (Vine Green, slightly yellowed) on the ground near the trellis bases, suggesting seasonal maintenance.
- **A wooden sign:** A small flat cuboid sign (Warm Brown) on a thin post near the shed, adding character to the scene.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Bright, warm directional sunlight from a high angle — this is a Mediterranean scene baking under golden light. The Earth Tan ground should glow warmly.
- The Grape Purple clusters should contrast richly against the Vine Green leaves — make sure the lighting emphasizes this color interplay.
- Soft ambient fill to keep shadows from going too dark — vineyards are open, airy places.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the shed should be visible at one end with the trellis rows extending outward, showing the full length of the vine lines.
- The overall atmosphere should feel sun-drenched, orderly, and productive — a place where careful cultivation produces something refined.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
