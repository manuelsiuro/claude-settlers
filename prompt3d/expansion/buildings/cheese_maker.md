# Cheese Maker — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Cheese Maker"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Cheese Maker is a **resource processing building** (Tier 3) — it employs a Cheese Maker who transforms Milk into Cheese. Cheese is a valuable food and trade good. The building should look like a cozy stone cottage with cheese wheels proudly displayed outside — an artisanal, inviting workshop.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be artisanal, cozy, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Cheese Maker. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The main building:** A small, squat stone cottage (Stone Grey cuboid walls). This is a compact workshop — keep it modestly sized, suggesting a single craftsperson's operation. Add thin cuboid trim (Warm Brown) along the corners and base to suggest timber framing reinforcing the stone.
- **The roof:** A low-pitched two-plane roof (Dark Brown) with slightly overhanging eaves. Add a small, simple chimney (Stone Grey cuboid) on one end — the Cheese Maker needs gentle heat for curdling.
- **The wide front:** The front face should have a large open section or wide window — a display/sales counter area. Model this as a recessed cuboid opening with a flat cuboid counter shelf (Cream) at waist height. Behind the counter, the interior should be visible (a dark plane or Cream-colored back wall).
- **The display area:** On and around the front counter, place 2–3 cheese wheels (Pale Yellow short, wide cylinders) — this is the building's signature visual. These golden discs immediately identify the building's function.
- **A side door:** A modest rectangular door (Warm Brown) on one side face for the worker to enter.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key cheese maker elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Cheese wheels:** The star props. Model 4–5 short, wide cylinders (Pale Yellow) of varying sizes. Place 2–3 on the front display counter, one on an exterior shelf, and one cut into a wedge shape (use a boolean cut or simply model a triangular prism) to show the inside. The bright yellow color against the grey stone is the building's visual identity.
- **Wax-sealed rounds:** 1–2 cheese wheels with a Wax Red coating — use the same cylinder shape but with the Wax Red color to suggest aged, premium cheese. Place these on a high shelf or in a prominent position.
- **Milk jugs (input):** 2–3 small cylinders with narrow necks (Cream or Milk White) placed near the side entrance — the raw material being delivered from the Dairy Farm.
- **Cheese press:** A small mechanical prop near the side of the building — a flat cuboid base with two vertical cuboid uprights and a horizontal cross-beam on top, with a cylinder pressing plate that slides down. This is the key tool of the trade.
- **Aging rack:** A small wooden shelf structure (Warm Brown cuboid frame with 2–3 horizontal shelf planes) placed against the exterior side wall, holding additional cheese wheels at various stages of aging. Arrange them in neat rows.
- **A wooden cutting board:** A small flat cuboid (Warm Brown) on the counter with a cheese wedge and a tiny flat blade (steel grey) — suggesting active preparation.
- **Cloth wrappings:** Tiny flat cuboid pieces (Cream) draped over some cheese wheels — cheesecloth used in the making process.
- **A small herb planter:** A cuboid planter (Warm Brown) near the door with tiny green cone shapes — herbs used to flavor certain cheeses.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm, soft directional sunlight that makes the Pale Yellow cheese wheels glow golden — they should be the brightest, most eye-catching elements in the scene.

- The Stone Grey walls provide a neutral backdrop that lets the yellow cheese and Wax Red accents pop with color contrast.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
