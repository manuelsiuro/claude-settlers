# Dairy Farm — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Dairy Farm"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Dairy Farm is a **resource gathering building** (Tier 2) — it employs a Dairymaid who feeds cows with Grain and collects Milk. Milk feeds into the Cheese Maker for processed goods. The Dairy Farm should look like a clean, white-walled farmhouse with a small fenced pasture beside it — a pastoral, wholesome scene.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be pastoral, clean, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Farmhouse walls** — White (`#F0F0F0`)
- **Roof and shutters** — Light Brown (`#C4A56E`)
- **Door, beams, and trim** — Warm Brown (`#A0724A`)
- **Pasture grass** — Green Pasture (`#7CBA5C`)
- **Fence rails and posts** — Fence Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Milk pails and liquid** — Milk White (`#FAFAFA`)
- **Cow body** — Cow Brown (`#8B5E3C`)

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Dairy Farm. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The farmhouse:** A small, clean cuboid building (White walls) with a low, gently pitched roof (Light Brown). The white walls are the building's signature — they communicate cleanliness and dairy production. Add thin cuboid trim pieces (Warm Brown) along the base and corners for a half-timbered look.
- **The roof:** A simple two-plane pitched roof (Light Brown) that overhangs slightly on all sides. Add a small flat cuboid chimney (Warm Brown) on one end — modest, not prominent.
- **The pasture:** A flat area of Green Pasture grass adjacent to the farmhouse, enclosed by a wooden fence. This should be roughly the same footprint as the building itself — a compact but visible grazing area. Use a slightly raised flat cuboid plane with a brighter green than the surrounding terrain.
- **The fence:** Cuboid posts (Fence Brown) with two horizontal rails on all sides of the pasture. Add a small gate section on the side nearest the farmhouse door — two posts slightly closer together with a horizontal rail that suggests a swinging gate.
- **The door:** A wide, arched-top door (Warm Brown) on the face adjacent to the pasture — the Dairymaid moves between the house and the animals frequently.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key dairy farm elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Milk pails:** 2–3 small tapered cylinders (Milk White, slightly wider at the top) with thin wire handles (a half-torus arc on top). Place them near the farmhouse door — one upright and full (add a tiny white disc on top for the milk surface), one tipped slightly as if just set down.
- **Cow silhouette:** A simplified cow shape in the pasture — a rectangular cuboid body (Cow Brown) with four short cylinder legs, a smaller cuboid head with two tiny cone horns, and a thin cylinder tail. Add irregular white patches (flat cuboid decals) on the body for a classic dairy cow look. Keep it very low-poly — this is a background element, not the focus.
- **Hay bale:** A short, wide cylinder (golden tan) placed inside the pasture near the fence — feed for the cows.
- **Grain trough:** A long, low, open-topped cuboid (Warm Brown) placed inside the pasture. This is where the Grain input is consumed — the cows eat from here. Add a few tiny golden spheres inside to suggest grain.
- **Milking stool:** A tiny three-legged stool (Warm Brown) — a short cylinder seat on three thin angled cylinder legs — placed near the cow, telling the story of the Dairymaid's daily work.
- **A butter churn (optional):** A tall, narrow cylinder (Warm Brown) with a thin vertical stick poking out the top, placed near the farmhouse door — suggesting additional dairy processing.
- **Flower boxes:** Small rectangular cuboid planters (Warm Brown) mounted below the windows, filled with tiny colorful sphere flowers — the Dairy Farm is a tidy, well-kept place.
- **Grass tufts:** Small cone shapes (Green Pasture, varied heights) scattered around the pasture edges and along the fence line.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Bright, clean directional sunlight — this is a wholesome daytime scene. The White walls should glow softly, and the Green Pasture should feel lush and fresh.
- A gentle warm ambient fill to keep the pastoral mood without harsh shadows.
- The white farmhouse walls and milk pails should be the brightest elements in the scene, immediately communicating "dairy."
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the farmhouse and adjacent pasture (with cow and fence) should both be fully visible, composing a complete little farmstead.
- The overall atmosphere should feel clean, productive, and peaceful — a well-run homestead where animals are cared for and good milk flows daily.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
