# Market — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Market"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Market is a **logistics building** — it auto-distributes food to nearby buildings within its radius, acting as a central hub for the settlement's food economy. It should look like a bustling open-air bazaar with colorful stall awnings and crates of goods, radiating commerce and activity.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be bustling, commercial, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Market. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The central pavilion:** An open-air post-and-beam structure — 4–6 vertical wooden posts (Dark Brown cylinders) supporting a large roof frame. No solid walls — the openness signals a public commercial space. The footprint is wide, one of the largest non-military buildings.
- **The roof / awning canopy:** A broad, gently sloped roof made of overlapping colorful cloth sections. Model as 3–4 flat angled planes in different colors (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow) — each awning section covers a different stall area beneath. The colorful patchwork roof is the Market's signature visual.
- **The raised platform:** A slightly elevated wooden platform floor (Warm Brown flat cuboid) — just one step above ground level. This defines the market area and keeps goods off the packed earth.
- **Stall dividers:** Low wooden counters or half-walls (Warm Brown cuboids, waist-height) dividing the space into 3–4 stall sections, each beneath a different awning color.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key market elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Goods crates:** Multiple small wooden crates (Crate Brown cuboids) stacked in 2s and 3s at each stall — representing the variety of goods being distributed.
- **Barrels:** 2–3 small barrels (Brown cylinders) near one stall — food and drink storage.
- **Food displays:** Tiny colored spheres and shapes on the stall counters — representing bread (golden), fruit (red/green), cheese (yellow) — showing the food distribution role.
- **A balance scale:** A tiny T-shaped prop (Brown stick with two small flat pans) on one counter — the classic merchant's tool.
- **Sacks and baskets:** Small off-white sacks (bulging cuboids) and woven baskets (brown cylinders) scattered around the stalls — goods awaiting distribution.
- **A signpost:** A tall wooden post (Dark Brown) near the entrance with a small horizontal board — marking this as the town market.
- **Cobblestone ground:** The packed earth around the Market has a slightly different texture (lighter, more worn) suggesting heavy foot traffic. A few flat grey stones embedded in the ground.
- **A central pillar or post:** One prominent central post (thicker than the others) in the middle of the pavilion, possibly with a small banner or flag.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Bright, even directional sunlight — the Market is an outdoor, daytime space. The colorful awnings should glow vibrantly.
- The multiple awning colors (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow) should create the most colorful building in the settlement — an eye-catching landmark visible from the minimap.
- Subtle warm ambient fill to make the goods and wood tones inviting.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the open layout with colorful awnings and busy stalls should all be visible from above.
- The Market should read as "the heart of commerce" — colorful, busy, and welcoming, contrasting with the serious tones of military buildings.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
