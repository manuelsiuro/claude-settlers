# Orchard — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Orchard"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Orchard is a **resource gathering building** (Tier 2) — it employs an Orchardist who tends fruit trees and harvests Fruit. The Orchard should look like a small rustic shed surrounded by a neat grid of round, leafy fruit trees heavy with red and orange fruit — a pastoral scene of abundance.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be pastoral, abundant, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Tree canopy** — Green Canopy (`#4A8C3F`)
- **Red fruit** — Red Fruit (`#CC3333`)
- **Orange fruit** — Orange Fruit (`#E08030`)
- **Shed walls and beams** — Warm Brown (`#A0724A`)
- **Shed roof and fence** — Dark Brown (`#5C3A1E`)
- **Grass and ground cover** — Light Green (`#7CBA5C`)
- **Trunk and branches** — Earth Brown (`#6B4226`)
- **Inner leaf highlights** — Leaf Green (`#5CA040`)

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Orchard. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The shed:** A small, low cuboid building (Warm Brown walls, Dark Brown pitched roof). This is a humble storage structure — keep it compact, about half the size of a standard building. Use a simple two-plane sloped roof. Add a wide door on the front face for moving baskets in and out.
- **The fruit trees:** 3–5 small round trees arranged in a tidy grid pattern adjacent to the shed. Each tree is a short, tapered cylinder trunk (Earth Brown) topped with a large icosphere or subdivided cube canopy (Green Canopy). Scale the canopies to be full and round — these are mature, well-tended trees. Scatter small sphere primitives (Red Fruit, Orange Fruit) across the surface of each canopy to represent ripe fruit hanging from branches.
- **The fence:** A low wooden fence (Dark Brown) made of thin cuboid posts with horizontal rail bars, enclosing the tree grid on 2–3 sides. The fence gives the orchard a managed, cultivated appearance.
- **The ground plane:** A flat area of Light Green grass beneath and around the trees. Add a subtle earth-toned path (a thin flat cuboid in Earth Brown) leading from the shed to the tree rows.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key orchard elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Fruit baskets:** 2–3 small open-topped cuboid or tapered cylinder baskets (Warm Brown) placed near the shed door, filled with tiny sphere fruit (Red and Orange) poking above the rim — freshly harvested produce ready for transport.
- **Picking ladder:** A long, thin ladder made from two parallel cylinders with short cuboid rungs (Warm Brown), leaning against one of the fruit trees at an angle. This immediately tells the story of the Orchardist at work.
- **Fallen fruit:** Several tiny spheres (Red Fruit, Orange Fruit) scattered on the ground beneath the trees — windfall fruit adds a naturalistic touch.
- **Tool shed shelf:** A small flat cuboid shelf attached to the outside wall of the shed, holding tiny props like a watering can (a small cylinder with a cone spout) and pruning shears (two tiny flat blades crossed).
- **Pruning shears:** A small prop near the shed — two thin flat cuboids joined at a pivot, representing the Orchardist's hand tool.
- **Grass tufts and flowers:** Small cone or diamond shapes (Light Green, Leaf Green) scattered around the base of the fence and along the path edges. Add a few tiny colorful flower dots for charm.
- **A wooden crate:** A small open cuboid crate (Dark Brown) stacked near the baskets, used for storing sorted fruit.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm, golden directional sunlight filtering through the canopies, casting gentle shadows on the grass below and making the green foliage glow.
- A soft ambient fill light to keep the pastoral mood bright and cheerful — this is a place of growth and plenty.
- The red and orange fruit should catch the light and stand out vividly against the green canopy — they are the visual focal point.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the shed, tree grid, and fence should all be visible, with the trees taking up the majority of the scene.
- The overall atmosphere should feel like a peaceful country garden — warm, productive, and bountiful.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
