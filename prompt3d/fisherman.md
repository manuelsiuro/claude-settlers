# Fisherman's Hut — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Fisherman's Hut"** in a low-poly 3D style using software like Blender or Maya.

The Fisherman's Hut is a **resource gathering building** — it employs a Fisherman who catches Fish from adjacent water tiles. It must be built next to water. It should look like a charming coastal or lakeside cottage with a small pier extending toward the water. The blue palette sets it apart from other buildings.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be charming, maritime, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Hut walls** — Light Blue (`#7EB3D8`)

- **Pier/dock planks** — Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Door and window frames** — Warm Brown (`#A0724A`)

- **Rope/net** — Beige (`#D0C0A0`)
- **Grass near shore** — Coastal Green (`#5A8F5A`)

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the main hut. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The hut:** A small cuboid (Light Blue). The blue color is the signature — it immediately communicates "water-related building." Keep the proportions compact and low.
- **The roof:** A simple four-sided pyramid sitting on top of the walls (Dark Blue). The darker blue roof on lighter blue walls creates a cohesive nautical palette.
- **The pier:** A thin, flat, elongated cuboid (Brown) extending from one side of the hut outward — representing a small dock reaching toward the water. This is a key visual element that shows the building's water connection. Add 2–3 thin vertical cuboid posts underneath for support.
- **The door:** A darker rectangular indentation on the wall facing away from the water (the land side).
- **A round window:** A small circular shape or octagonal cutout on the side wall — a porthole-like detail.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key fisherman elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Fishing rod:** A thin, long cylinder (Tan) leaning against the hut or resting on the pier. Add a tiny thread (thin cylinder) hanging from the tip.
- **Fish catch:** 1–2 small elongated, flattened ellipsoids or leaf shapes (Silver/Pink) on the pier or in a tiny crate — the building's output.
- **A bucket or barrel:** A small cylinder near the pier end, filled with the day's catch.
- **Fishing net:** A small draped shape (Beige) hanging on the hut's exterior wall or laid out on the pier — a thin, crudely modeled plane.
- **Water edge:** The scene should include a section of water — a flat plane (Teal Blue) with slight undulation. The pier extends over this water.
- **Shore details:** Some small rounded rocks (grey spheres/cubes) at the waterline. A few reeds — thin vertical cylinders with tiny leaf-shaped tops.
- **A small rowboat (optional):** A simple elongated hull shape tied to the pier — 2–3 cuboids forming a boat silhouette.
- **Grass tufts:** Low-poly grass near the shore (Coastal Green), becoming sparser closer to the water.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Bright sunlight with a slightly cool tint to evoke a lakeside or coastal atmosphere.
- A subtle blue ambient fill reflecting the water environment.
- Light reflections — a small bright spot on the water surface near the pier to suggest sun glinting off the waves.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — both the hut and the extending pier should be clearly visible, showing the land-to-water connection.
- The blue hut should stand out distinctively from the browns and greens of other buildings.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
