# Forester's Hut — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Forester's Hut"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Forester's Hut is a **resource gathering building** — it employs a Forester who plants new saplings to ensure a renewable supply of trees. It should look like a nature-loving woodland cottage with a green pyramid roof, surrounded by young saplings and greenery.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be natural, earthy, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Main wood walls** — Brown (`#8B5E3C`)

- **Sapling cone** — Bright Green (`#5DBF5D`)
- **Door and trim** — Warm Brown (`#A0724A`)
- **Foundation** — Earthy Brown (`#6B4226`)
- **Grass/ground** — Meadow Green (`#5A8F4A`)
- **Soil patches** — Dark Earth (`#5C3D2E`)
- **Tiny flowers** — Yellow (`#F0D060`) and White (`#F0F0E0`)

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the main hut. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The foundation:** A very low cuboid base (Earthy Brown), small footprint — this is a humble one-room hut.
- **The walls:** A small, low cuboid (Brown). Same scale as the Woodcutter's Hut — a compact forest dwelling.
- **The roof:** A simple four-sided pyramid sitting on top of the walls (Dark Green). The green roof is the key visual distinguisher from the Woodcutter — it signals the building's connection to planting and growing. The pyramid should slightly overhang the walls.
- **The door:** A darker rectangular indentation centered on one wall face.
- **A small window:** A tiny square cutout on the side, suggesting the Forester watches the forest from inside.

---

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key forester elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **The sapling:** The signature prop — a tiny cone (Bright Green) on a thin cylinder trunk next to the hut. This immediately tells the player "this building plants trees." Place it prominently near the entrance.
- **Additional saplings:** 2–3 more tiny cones at varying heights in the surrounding area, showing the Forester's work in progress — a mini nursery.
- **A small watering can or seed bag:** A tiny cuboid or cylinder prop near the door (Brown/Grey) to suggest planting tools.
- **Grass tufts and wildflowers:** Flattened low-poly grass clusters (Meadow Green) scattered around, with tiny colored cube "flowers" (Yellow, White) to give a garden-like feel.
- **Soil patches:** Small flat dark cuboids on the ground near saplings, representing freshly turned earth where trees were just planted.
- **A mature tree nearby:** One larger cone-on-cylinder tree to contrast with the tiny saplings — showing the lifecycle the Forester manages.
- **Optional fence:** Low wooden fence posts around a small sapling nursery area.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Bright, warm sunlight from above to illuminate the green roof and saplings vibrantly.
- A subtle green ambient fill to harmonize the scene's natural palette.
- The dark green roof should contrast clearly against the brown walls — this is the building's visual signature.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the tiny sapling next to the hut should be clearly readable, as it's the key identifier.
- The overall feel should be lush and hopeful — a building about growth and renewal.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
