# Harbor — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Harbor"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Harbor is a **logistics building** — it facilitates transport of goods over water, extending the logistics network across seas and rivers. It features a long pier extending from land into water, a small dock building, and simple boats. Used on maps with significant water bodies.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be maritime, functional, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Dock building walls** — Light Brown / Grey (`#B0A090`)
- **Dock building roof** — Dark Grey (`#5A5A5A`)
- **Boat hull** — Blue (`#3A6FAF`)

- **Crates and cargo** — Warm Brown (`#A0724A`)

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Harbor. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The pier:** A long, flat, dark brown cuboid (Dark Brown) extending from the land out into the water. This is the dominant structural element — make it visibly long and narrow. Place support posts (Brown vertical cuboids) at regular intervals underneath.
- **The dock building:** A small cuboid building (Light Brown/Grey) located at the land-end of the pier. This is where harbor workers manage cargo. Add a simple slightly sloped roof (Dark Grey).
- **The dock building door:** A wide doorway on the pier-facing side — wide enough for cargo to pass through.
- **Mooring posts:** Short, thick cylinders at the pier's edges — where boats tie up.
- **The water:** A flat plane (Teal Blue) surrounding the pier section. The pier extends over this water.
- **The shore:** Where the pier meets land, a sandy/gravel area (Sandy Beige) transitioning from the regular terrain.

---

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key harbor elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Boats (indicator):** 1–2 simple boat shapes docked alongside the pier:
  - **Hull:** An elongated, slightly tapered cuboid (Blue) — wider in the middle, narrower at the ends. The bottom should be slightly V-shaped.
  - **Cabin/deck:** A small central cuboid (Warm Brown) on top of the hull.
  - **Mast:** A thin vertical cylinder rising from the cabin, with a small flat triangular plane (White) as a simple sail.
- **Cargo crates:** Several small cubes (Warm Brown) stacked on the pier near the boats — goods being loaded/unloaded.
- **Barrels:** A few short cylinders on the pier — cargo containers.
- **Rope coils:** Tiny torus shapes or coiled cylinder shapes (Beige) on the mooring posts and pier surface.
- **A crane or loading arm (optional):** A simple boom — a diagonal cuboid with a rope (thin cylinder) hanging from the end — for loading heavy cargo onto boats.
- **Fishing nets:** A draped flat mesh shape (Beige) hanging on the dock building exterior.
- **A gangplank:** A thin flat cuboid bridging from the pier to a docked boat.
- **Seagulls (optional):** 2–3 tiny white V-shapes perched on the mooring posts — maritime atmosphere.
- **Shore rocks:** A few rounded grey shapes along the waterline.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Bright, slightly cool maritime sunlight — clear coastal atmosphere.
- The blue boats should stand out as colorful accents against the brown pier and grey dock building.
- Water reflections — a subtle bright highlight on the teal water surface near the pier.
- A cool ambient fill reflecting the maritime environment.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the full length of the pier extending from land to water should be visible, with boats docked alongside.
- The overall atmosphere should feel bustling and maritime — a gateway for trade across the water.
- The transition from land (sandy shore) through the dock building and along the pier to the boats tells a clear story of goods moving from land to sea.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
