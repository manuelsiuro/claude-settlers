# Slaughterhouse — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Slaughterhouse"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Slaughterhouse is a **resource processing building** — it processes Pigs (from the Pig Farm) into Meat, which feeds Miners. The building should look utilitarian and somewhat imposing with its dark red/maroon color palette — clearly a working meat-processing facility.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be functional, sturdy, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Slaughterhouse. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The main building:** A medium cuboid (Dark Red/Maroon). The dark red color immediately distinguishes it from other processing buildings and hints at its purpose. Medium footprint — a solid, industrial structure.
- **The roof:** A simple sloped cuboid roof (Dark Brown) — a two-plane pitched shape with slight overhang.
- **The door:** A wide, dark rectangular indentation — large enough for animals to enter. The door should be on the front face.
- **The walls:** Thick and solid-looking — add subtle beam cross-pieces (Dark Brown) on the exterior to suggest reinforced construction.
- **A rear loading area:** A slightly lower, flat cuboid extension at the back — where processed meat is stored/collected.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key slaughterhouse elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **The chopping block:** A small grey cube (Grey) near the entrance — the iconic butcher's block. This is the signature prop that immediately communicates the building's function.
- **A cleaver/butcher's tool:** A small T-shaped tool (Steel Grey blade, Dark Brown handle) embedded in or resting on the chopping block.
- **Meat pieces (output):** 1–2 small red-pink cuboids or rounded shapes near the back of the building — processed meat ready for transport to the mines.
- **Hooks:** 1–2 small, thin bent shapes (Steel Grey) hanging from the roof overhang or a horizontal beam — meat hooks, suggesting the processing trade.
- **Barrels:** Small cylinders (Brown) near the rear — for storing and transporting meat.
- **A blood stain (subtle):** A small, very flat dark crimson shape on the ground near the chopping block. Keep it subtle and tasteful — just enough to tell the story.
- **An animal pen/holding area:** A small fence section near the entrance where pigs wait — 2–3 fence posts with rails.
- **A bucket:** Small cylinder (grey) near the chopping block.
- **Sparse surroundings:** Minimal vegetation near the building — the ground is trampled and utilitarian.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Moderate directional sunlight — not too warm, not too cool. The slaughterhouse has a businesslike atmosphere.
- The dark red walls should look rich but not garish — avoid making it look like a horror scene. This is a functional medieval building, not gruesome.
- Good contrast between the maroon walls, dark brown roof, and grey chopping block.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the chopping block and entrance should be clearly visible.
- A cool ambient fill to balance the warm reds and prevent the scene from feeling too intense.
- The overall atmosphere should feel sturdy and professional — an essential part of the food chain, not sinister.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
