# Warehouse / Storehouse — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Warehouse / Storehouse"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Warehouse (or Storehouse) is a **logistics building** — it provides centralized storage for surplus goods. Transporters pick up and drop off goods here. It should look like a large, utilitarian storage barn with multiple loading doors — the biggest civilian building after the Castle.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be spacious, functional, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Warehouse. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The main building:** A long, wide, plain cuboid (Light Brown/Beige). This is a large-footprint building — wider and longer than processing buildings. Think barn or warehouse proportions — elongated rectangle.
- **The roof:** A simple, large, slightly sloped cuboid roof (Darker Brown/Grey) covering the entire structure. A gentle two-plane pitch with slight overhang.
- **Loading doors:** Several wide, darker rectangular indentations (Dark Brown) along the long sides — loading bays where transporters deliver goods. 2–3 doors on each long wall to show the high volume of goods flowing through.
- **The foundation:** A low, wide cuboid (Grey) — a solid stone platform. The warehouse needs a good foundation for heavy loads.
- **Cross-beams:** Thin flat cuboids on the exterior walls in an X or half-timber pattern — structural reinforcement for the large building.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key warehouse elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Crates (prominent):** Multiple small cubes and cuboids (Warm Brown) stacked near the loading doors — wooden storage crates of various sizes. This is the signature visual — a warehouse surrounded by goods.
- **Barrels:** Several short cylinders (Brown) grouped near the doors — storage containers for various resources.
- **Sacks:** Rounded cuboid shapes (Burlap Tan) piled alongside the crates — grain, flour, or other bulk goods.
- **A loading platform:** A flat cuboid (wood) extending from one of the doors — a raised platform for loading/unloading from wagons.
- **A hand cart or wheelbarrow:** A small wheeled prop near the door for moving goods.
- **Organized staging areas:** The goods should be somewhat organized — crates on one side, barrels on another — suggesting efficient warehouse management.
- **A sign or manifest board:** A tiny flat cuboid (Parchment) pinned near the main entrance — inventory records.
- **Ground markings:** The area around the warehouse should be packed earth (not grass) — heavy foot and cart traffic.
- **A hitching post:** A thin horizontal cuboid on posts — where transportation animals or carts wait.

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- The Light Brown/Beige walls should look clean and well-maintained — this is a well-run facility.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the long building with its multiple doors and surrounding goods should read clearly as a storage facility.
