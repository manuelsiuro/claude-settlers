# Geologist's Hut — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Geologist's Hut"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Geologist's Hut is a **resource gathering building** — it employs a Geologist who prospects mountain tiles to discover ore deposits (Iron, Coal, Gold, Stone). It is the smallest building in the game — a tiny surveyor's shack with a distinctive bright yellow marker cone. Think of a prospector's base camp.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be compact, exploratory, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Hut walls** — Dark Brown (`#5C3A1E`)
- **Flat roof** — Dark Brown (`#4A2E14`)
- **Marker cone** — Bright Yellow (`#F0C030`)
- **Ground/mountain terrain** — Rocky Grey (`#8A8A8A`)
- **Map/scroll** — Parchment (`#E8D8B8`)
- **Hammer/tool metal** — Steel Grey (`#6A6A6A`)
- **Tool handle** — Tan (`#C4A060`)
- **Ore sample cubes** — Red (`#C0392B`), Black (`#2A2A2A`), Yellow (`#DAA520`)

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Geologist's Hut. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The hut:** A very small cuboid (Dark Brown) — this is the tiniest building in the game. Just a one-person shack barely big enough for a desk and cot.
- **The flat roof:** A flat cuboid on top, same footprint as the walls (Dark Brown, slightly different shade). No pitched roof — just a simple, utilitarian flat top.
- **The marker cone:** A small, bright yellow cone placed on top of the roof or prominently beside the hut. This is the key visual identifier — a prospector's survey marker. It should be clearly visible and brightly colored.
- **The door:** A tiny dark rectangular indentation. The door is small — the hut is barely person-height.

---

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key geologist elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Survey markers:** 2–3 additional tiny yellow cones placed on the ground at various distances from the hut, as if the Geologist has been marking prospecting sites.
- **Ore samples:** Very small cubes in different colors near the hut entrance — a Red cube (iron ore), a Black cube (coal), a Yellow cube (gold ore) — showing what the Geologist searches for.
- **A map or scroll:** A tiny flat cuboid (Parchment) on the ground or pinned to the hut wall — suggesting geological surveys.
- **A prospecting hammer:** A small T-shaped tool (Steel Grey head, Tan handle) leaning against the hut.
- **Rock specimens:** A few small irregular grey cubes arranged near the door like a sample collection.
- **Rocky terrain:** The ground should feel mountainous — irregular grey cuboids of varying heights suggesting rocky mountain terrain. The Geologist works near mountains.
- **A magnifying glass (optional):** A tiny circle on a stick — a fun detail suggesting mineral examination.
- **Sparse vegetation:** Very little greenery — mostly bare rock and gravel at this altitude.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Clear, bright high-altitude light — strong directional sun with crisp shadows.
- Cool ambient fill suggesting mountain air.
- The bright yellow marker cone should pop dramatically against the dark brown hut and grey rocky terrain — it's the building's most distinctive element.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the tiny hut should look appropriately small, nestled among rocks.
- The overall atmosphere should feel rugged and exploratory — a lone prospector's outpost on the mountainside.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
