# Gold Mine — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Gold Mine"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Gold Mine is a **resource gathering building** — it employs a Miner who extracts Gold Ore from rare mountain deposits. Gold Ore is processed by the Goldsmith into Gold Bars, which provide a global combat bonus to all Knights. The Gold Mine shares the general mine layout but features a distinctive yellow/gold ore indicator sphere.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be prestigious, mountain-hewn, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Mine entrance structure** — Dark Grey (`#5A5A5A`)
- **Mine shaft opening** — Very Dark / Black (`#1A1A1A`)
- **Gold ore indicator** — Bright Yellow / Gold (`#DAA520`)
- **Mountain rock** — Rocky Grey (`#8A8A8A`) and Warm Grey (`#9A8A7A`)
- **Support timber beams** — Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Gold ore nuggets** — Gold (`#DAA520`) with Pale Gold highlights (`#F0D060`)
- **Ground/dirt** — Mountain Earth (`#7A6A5A`)
- **Ore cart** — Dark Wood (`#5C3A1E`)
- **Rail track** — Dark Steel (`#4A4A4A`)

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Gold Mine. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The mountain backdrop:** A large angular mass of cuboids (Rocky Grey, Warm Grey) — slightly warmer grey tones than other mines, hinting at gold-bearing quartz veins.
- **The mine entrance structure:** A dark grey cuboid (Dark Grey) built into the mountain face — the reinforced entrance. Same general proportions as other mines.
- **The mine shaft opening:** A dark rectangle on the front face — the tunnel going deep into gold-bearing rock.
- **Support timber beams:** Thin cuboids (Brown) framing the shaft opening — well-maintained beams, suggesting a valuable mine worth investing in.
- **The gold ore indicator:** A small yellow sphere (Gold `#DAA520`) prominently placed near the entrance. The round, glowing shape distinguishes this from the other mines' cube indicators and suggests a precious nugget.

---

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key gold mine elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Gold ore nuggets:** A few small yellow spheres and irregular cubes (Gold, Pale Gold) near the entrance — the precious output. Even a small pile feels valuable.
- **Ore cart:** A small cart (Dark Wood) on rail tracks, containing a few gold nuggets — noticeably fewer than the coal or iron mines, suggesting gold's rarity.
- **Rail tracks:** Two thin parallel cuboids extending from the entrance.
- **A gold vein in the rock (optional):** A thin yellow streak (flat cuboid) visible in the mountain face near the entrance — showing the geological deposit.
- **Pickaxe and pan:** A pickaxe leaning against the entrance, and a small circular disc (a gold panning pan) on the ground.
- **Lantern:** A warm-glowing lantern near the entrance.
- **Sacks or crates:** Small cuboids near the entrance for storing and transporting the precious ore.
- **Rocky terrain:** Mountain terrain with warm-toned greys. Slightly more hospitable-looking than the coal mine.
- **Sparse mountain vegetation:** A few hardy shrubs (small green spheres on sticks).

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm directional sunlight to make the gold ore nuggets and indicator sphere gleam.
- Subtle warm ambient fill — the gold mine should feel "warmer" than the coal or iron mines.
- A small golden-orange accent light near the gold pile to make it feel precious.
- The yellow gold indicator should be the most eye-catching element — shining against the grey mountain backdrop.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the gold nuggets and yellow sphere should be immediately visible.
- The overall atmosphere should feel rare and valuable — this is a prized resource site.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
