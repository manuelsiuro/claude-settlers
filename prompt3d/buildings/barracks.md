# Barracks / Fortress — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Barracks / Fortress"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Barracks (or Fortress) is the **major military building** — it houses 10–15+ Knights, provides the largest territorial expansion, and is the most imposing military structure after the Castle. It should look like a smaller version of the Castle — a wide, fortified stronghold with optional corner towers and red flags.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be massive, fortified, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Barracks. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The main structure:** A large, wide, medium-height cuboid (Dark Grey/Black). This is the second-largest building footprint in the game after the Castle. Wide and imposing — a true fortress.
- **Corner towers (optional but recommended):** Smaller square cuboids at the corners, slightly taller than the main structure (Dark Grey). Topped with small red pyramids (Red) — echoing the Castle's design language.
- **Crenellations:** Small cube merlons along the top edges of the main structure and towers — classic medieval battlements.
- **The gate:** A large, dark rectangular opening on the front face (Very Dark Grey) — wide enough for troops to march through. Larger and more imposing than the Guard Hut's door.
- **The roof:** Flat top (it's a fortress, not a house) with crenellated edges.
- **Arrow slits:** Multiple thin rectangular indentations on all walls.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key barracks elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Red flags:** Red pyramidal flags (Red `#C0392B`) on any towers or prominent corners. Multiple flags — this is a major military installation.
- **Weapon racks (prominent):** Large wooden frames (Brown) near the entrance displaying multiple swords and shields — more weapons than the Guard Hut, showing the larger garrison.
- **A training yard:** A flat, cleared area in front of the barracks with a training dummy (T-shaped wooden figure) and weapon racks — where knights practice.
- **Banners:** Long rectangular fabric planes (Deep Red) hanging from the walls — more decorative than the Guard Hut, showing the prestige of the fortress.
- **Torches:** Multiple torch sconces (Warm Orange flames) along the walls — the barracks is well-lit.
- **Defensive elements:** Stone barriers (grey cuboids), wooden stakes, and possibly a small wall section around the perimeter.
- **Supply crates:** Several cubes and barrels near the entrance — the barracks stores military supplies.
- **A watchtower element:** A slightly raised platform or lookout point at one corner.
- **Trampled ground:** The entire surrounding area is packed earth (Trampled Earth) — heavy foot traffic from a large garrison.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Strong, dramatic directional light casting sharp shadows — the Barracks commands attention.
- The red flags and banners should pop against the dark grey/black walls — the only warm colors in an otherwise stern scene.
- Multiple warm point lights from the torch sconces to add life and warmth to the fortress.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the full imposing width of the fortress should be visible.
- The Barracks should read as "smaller Castle" — clearly the most powerful military structure a player can build.
- The overall atmosphere should feel powerful, martial, and commanding — the stronghold of a conquering lord.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
