# Quarry — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Quarry"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Quarry is a **resource gathering building** — it employs a Stonemason who extracts Stone from surface deposits. It should look like a rough, open-fronted shelter built against a rocky outcrop, with stone blocks scattered nearby. The vibe is industrial, rugged, and utilitarian.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be rugged, utilitarian, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Shelter structure** — Grey (`#808080`)
- **Stone blocks (output)** — Light Grey (`#B8B8B8`)
- **Rocky terrain/outcrop** — Varied Greys (`#9A9A9A`, `#7A7A7A`, `#B0B0B0`)
- **Wooden support beams** — Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Ground/dirt** — Sandy Brown (`#A08060`)
- **Pickaxe metal** — Dark Steel (`#5A5A5A`)
- **Pickaxe handle** — Tan (`#C4A060`)
- **Dust/rubble** — Beige (`#D0C0A0`)

---

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Quarry. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The rocky outcrop:** Several jagged cuboids of varying sizes clustered together (Varied Greys) — these represent the raw stone deposit the building is built against. Make them angular and irregular to suggest natural rock formations. Place them at the back/side of the scene.
- **The shelter:** A small, open-fronted cuboid shelter (Grey) built against the rock face. It has three walls and a roof but the front is open — this is where the Stonemason works. The roof can be a simple flat or slightly angled cuboid.
- **Wooden support beams:** Thin cuboid posts (Brown) at the open front of the shelter, suggesting structural support for the rocky overhang.
- **The ground platform:** A slightly uneven terrain base with a mix of dirt and gravel tones.

---

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key quarry elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Stone blocks (output goods):** A few loose medium-sized cubes (Light Grey) placed outside the shelter opening — these are the finished product, cut stone blocks ready for transport.
- **Raw stone chunks:** Irregularly sized and rotated cubes near the rock face, suggesting stone in various stages of extraction.
- **Pickaxe:** A small T-shaped prop (thin cuboid handle + flat wedge head) leaning against the shelter wall or stuck into a stone block.
- **Stone dust and rubble:** Tiny scattered cubes and flat shapes (Beige) around the work area to suggest active quarrying.
- **A chisel and mallet (optional):** Tiny prop tools near the stone blocks.
- **A simple wooden wheelbarrow or sled:** A small boxy shape near the finished blocks, suggesting how stone gets transported to the flag.
- **Sparse ground vegetation:** Very minimal — a few dry grass tufts. Quarries are dusty, barren work sites.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use a strong directional light from above to create sharp shadows in the shelter opening and between the rock formations.
- A subtle warm ambient fill to bring out the various grey tones without flattening them.
- The contrast between the shelter structure and the jagged natural rock should be clear — manmade vs. natural.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the open-fronted shelter and stone block pile should be immediately readable.
- The overall palette is intentionally muted greys — let the geometric shapes do the visual storytelling.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
