# Farm — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Farm"** in a low-poly 3D style using software like Blender or Maya.

The Farm is a **resource gathering building** — it employs a Farmer who grows and harvests Grain. The Grain is used to feed the Windmill (for Flour) and the Pig Farm. The Farm should look like a rustic farmhouse with adjacent golden fields — a warm, pastoral scene.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be warm, pastoral, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Farmhouse walls** — Beige / Light Brown (`#C8A882`)

- **Ripe grain fields** — Golden Yellow (`#DAA520`)
- **Growing crop fields** — Crop Green (`#6B8E23`)

- **Door and beams** — Warm Brown (`#A0724A`)
- **Fence posts** — Brown (`#8B5E3C`)

- **Grass border** — Meadow Green (`#5A8F4A`)

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Farm. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The farmhouse:** A small, long, low cuboid (Beige/Light Brown). The farmhouse is stretched along one axis — longer than it is wide — giving it a distinctive barn-like silhouette.
- **The roof:** A simple sloped cuboid roof (Red-Brown) — a two-plane pitched shape resting on top of the farmhouse. The red-brown color is the building's signature identifier.
- **The door:** A wider-than-usual rectangular indentation on the long face — a barn-style entrance.
- **Exposed beams:** Thin flat cuboids (Warm Brown) on the walls in a half-timber pattern for visual interest.
- **The fields:** Adjacent flat area made of very low, flat cuboids representing crop rows. Mix Golden Yellow (ripe grain, ready to harvest) and Crop Green (growing) sections to show the agricultural cycle. The fields are the largest visual element — they take up more space than the building itself.
- **Field furrows:** Thin Dark Brown lines between crop rows to suggest tilled earth.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key farm elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Grain sheaves:** 2–3 small bundled shapes (tiny upright cylinders with wider tops) in Golden Yellow placed near the farmhouse — harvested grain ready for transport.
- **A scythe:** The Farmer's tool — a thin cylinder handle with a curved flat blade (Steel Grey) leaning against the farmhouse wall.
- **Hay bales:** 1–2 small cuboids or cylinders (straw-colored) near the building.
- **Fence:** Low wooden fence posts (Brown) with horizontal rails enclosing the field area on 2–3 sides.
- **A water trough or well:** A small cuboid trough (Grey) near the farmhouse.
- **Grass border:** Meadow Green grass tufts along the edges where field meets natural ground.
- **Optional scarecrow:** A T-shaped stick figure in the field — thin cylinders for body and arms with a tiny cuboid hat.
- **A cart or wheelbarrow:** Small boxy shape for transporting grain bundles.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm, golden-hour directional sunlight to make the grain fields glow and feel abundant.
- A soft warm ambient fill to keep the pastoral mood.
- The golden yellow fields should be the most eye-catching element, shimmering with warm light.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the farmhouse and fields should both be visible, with the fields taking up a significant portion of the scene.
- The overall atmosphere should feel productive, warm, and peaceful — the backbone of the food economy.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
