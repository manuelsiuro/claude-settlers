# Baker — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Baker"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Baker is a **resource processing serf** — working at the Bakery to bake Bread from Flour and Water. This character is one of the most visually distinctive serfs thanks to the tall white chef's hat and white apron. The Baker should look cheerful, clean, and ready to work.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be clean, culinary, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Baker character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The chef's hat:** A tall white cylinder (White) placed on top of the head. The hat should be approximately 50–60% of the head's height, making the Baker one of the tallest-silhouette serfs. The flat top of the cylinder is the classic chef's toque shape.
- **The limbs (simplified):** Arms extend forward, optionally holding a bread loaf. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Baker's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The chef's hat (co-primary identifier):** A tall cylinder (White) sitting on top of the head. This is the Baker's most iconic feature — visible from any angle and unique among all serfs. The hat adds significant height to the character's silhouette, making the Baker easy to spot.
- **The white apron (co-primary identifier):** A flat cuboid (White) attached to the front of the body, extending from chest to below the waist. Slightly narrower than the body. Combined with the hat, the white-on-brown color scheme is unmistakably a baker.
- **Bread loaf (optional carried item):** A small ovoid or rounded cuboid (Golden Brown) held between extended arms. This represents the Baker's output product. The warm golden color provides a nice contrast against the white apron.
- **No tools:** The Baker carries no tools — the hat and apron are sufficient visual identifiers.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and make the white hat and apron glow.
- A subtle warm ambient fill to give the whites a slightly baked, cozy feel.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The tall white chef's hat should be the most visually striking element from the isometric game camera angle — it adds unique height to this serf's silhouette.
- The white hat + white apron combination against the light brown body should be immediately recognizable.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
