# Pig Farmer — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Pig Farmer"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Pig Farmer is a **resource gathering serf** — raising pigs at the Pig Farm. This character tends livestock and should look earthy, practical, and a bit muddy. The defining visual is a wooden bucket used for feeding, along with muddy patches on the lower body.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be earthy, pastoral, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Pig Farmer character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to the side, carrying the bucket by its handle. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Pig Farmer's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The wooden bucket (primary identifier):** A small cylinder (Warm Brown) with an open top, approximately 30% of the body width. Add a tiny arched handle made from a thin curved cylinder or a half-torus (Tan) spanning the top of the bucket. The bucket is carried in one hand at the character's side — it represents the feed bucket used for the pigs.
- **Mud patches (secondary identifier):** 2–3 small flat cuboids (Dark Brown) applied to the lower portion of the body cylinder. These suggest the muddy conditions of working in a pig pen and add visual interest to the otherwise plain body.
- **No hat or special headwear:** The Pig Farmer is a humble profession — keep the design grounded and practical.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and the bucket shape.
- A subtle warm ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The bucket and mud patches should be clearly visible from the isometric game camera angle, communicating the Pig Farmer's livestock-tending role.
- The dark brown mud patches against the light brown body add texture and character to this otherwise simple design.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
