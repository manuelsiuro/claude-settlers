# Butcher — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Butcher"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Butcher is a **resource processing serf** — working at the Slaughterhouse to process Pigs into Meat. This character should look bold and distinctive thanks to the dark red/maroon apron — a unique color among all serfs. Optionally carries a cleaver for extra visual impact.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be bold, professional, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Butcher character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to the side holding the cleaver. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Butcher's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The maroon apron (primary identifier):** A flat cuboid (Maroon `#8B1A1A`) attached to the front of the body, extending from chest to below the waist. This dark red apron is the Butcher's most striking feature — Maroon is a unique color in the serf palette, making the Butcher immediately identifiable even at a distance. The apron is slightly narrower than the body.
- **The cleaver (secondary identifier):** A small, flat rectangular cuboid blade (Steel Grey) with a short cylinder handle (Tan). The cleaver is broader and shorter than a sword — a chunky, rectangular blade. Hold it in one hand at the character's side. This is optional but recommended for extra visual clarity.
- **No hat or special headwear:** The Butcher relies on the bold maroon color for identification.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and make the maroon apron richly colored.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The dark maroon apron should provide the strongest color contrast of any serf — it should immediately draw the eye from the isometric game camera angle.
- The cleaver's steel grey catches the light, adding a secondary visual accent.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
