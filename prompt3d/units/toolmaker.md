# Toolmaker — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Toolmaker"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Toolmaker is a **resource processing serf** — working at the Toolmaker's Workshop to craft Tools from Iron Bars and Planks. Tools are essential for assigning serfs to specialized jobs. This character should look practical, skilled, and equipped — carrying a toolbox and wearing a work apron.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be practical, skilled, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Toolmaker character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to the side carrying the toolbox. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Toolmaker's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The toolbox (primary identifier):** A small cuboid (Warm Brown `#8B5E3C`) carried in one hand at the character's side. Approximately 35% of the body width and slightly taller than wide — a compact wooden box. Optionally add a tiny handle on top — a thin cuboid or cylinder (Steel Grey). The toolbox represents the variety of tools this serf produces.
- **Work apron (secondary identifier):** A flat cuboid (Dark Brown `#5C3A1E`) attached to the front of the body, extending from chest to below the waist. Similar to the Builder's apron but paired with the toolbox instead of a hammer.
- **Tool metal accents (optional):** Tiny Steel Grey cuboids or cylinders poking out of the top of the toolbox — suggesting hammers, chisels, or other small tools inside. This adds visual interest but should be kept very simple at this scale.
- **No hat or special headwear:** The Toolmaker relies on the toolbox and apron for visual identification.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and the toolbox shape.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The toolbox and dark apron should be clearly visible from the isometric game camera angle, communicating the Toolmaker's crafting role.
- The warm brown toolbox against the dark brown apron and light brown body creates a layered brown palette unique to this profession.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
