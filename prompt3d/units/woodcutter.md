# Woodcutter — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Woodcutter"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Woodcutter is a **resource gathering serf** — chopping down nearby trees to produce Wood (Logs). This character works outdoors among the forests and should look rugged and ready to swing an axe. The axe is the defining visual element.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be rugged, outdoorsy, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Body / Clothing** — Light Brown (`#C4A56E`)
- **Head / Skin** — Light Peach (`#F5D5B8`)
- **Axe blade** — Dark Grey (`#5A5A5A`)
- **Axe handle** — Tan (`#D2A86E`)

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Woodcutter character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to the side holding the axe. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Woodcutter's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The axe (primary identifier):** A flat cuboid blade (Dark Grey) attached to a thin cylinder handle (Tan). The blade should be a flattened wedge shape — wider at the cutting edge, narrower where it meets the handle. The axe head is approximately 25–30% of the body width. Attach it to one arm, resting over the shoulder or held at the side ready to swing.
- **No special attire:** The Woodcutter wears only the base light brown clothing — no apron, no hat. The axe alone is the distinguishing feature, keeping the design clean and immediately readable.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and catch the axe blade.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The dark grey axe blade should provide strong contrast against the light brown body, making the Woodcutter instantly recognizable from the isometric game camera angle.
- The axe should be clearly visible even at small on-screen sizes.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
