# Fisherman — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Fisherman"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Fisherman is a **resource gathering serf** — catching Fish from nearby water tiles. This character works along rivers and coastlines and has a distinctly aquatic theme with light blue accents and a long fishing rod. The character should look patient, relaxed, and water-associated.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be calm, water-themed, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Body / Clothing** — Light Brown (`#C4A56E`)

- **Blue accent band** — Light Blue (`#7EB3D8`)
- **Fishing rod** — Tan (`#D2A86E`)

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Fisherman character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends upward and to the side, holding the fishing rod at an angle. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Fisherman's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The fishing rod (primary identifier):** A long, thin cylinder (Tan), held at an angle extending upward from one arm. The rod should be approximately 1.5× the character's height for a dramatic silhouette. Optionally, a very thin line (Light Blue) dangles from the tip — this can be a thin cylinder or omitted if too small to read at game scale.
- **Light blue accent band:** A thin cylinder or torus (Light Blue) wrapped around the body at chest height, or a small flat cuboid patch (Light Blue) on the chest. This water-colored accent ties the Fisherman to their aquatic role and provides the only color that distinguishes them from other rod/pole-carrying characters.
- **No hat or heavy tools:** The Fisherman is a simple design — the tall angled rod and blue accent do all the visual work.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and the angled fishing rod.
- A subtle cool-tinted ambient fill to hint at a waterside atmosphere.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The tall angled fishing rod creates a distinctive silhouette from the isometric game camera angle — it should be the tallest element and immediately readable.
- The light blue accent provides a unique color splash among the mostly brown/grey serf palette.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
