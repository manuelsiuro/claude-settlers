# Farmer — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Farmer"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Farmer is a **resource gathering serf** — working at a Farm, planting and harvesting Grain. This character works the fields under the open sky and is one of the most visually distinctive serfs thanks to the wide-brimmed hat and long scythe. The character should look pastoral, weathered, and hardworking.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be rustic, pastoral, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Body / Clothing** — Light Brown (`#C4A56E`)
- **Head / Skin** — Light Peach (`#F5D5B8`)
- **Wide-brimmed hat** — Light Brown (`#C4A56E`) or slightly darker Warm Brown (`#A0724A`)
- **Scythe blade** — Steel Grey (`#7A7A7A`)
- **Scythe handle** — Tan (`#D2A86E`)

---

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Farmer character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The hat:** A wide, flat cylinder or very shallow cone (Warm Brown) placed on top of the head — extending well past the head's width on all sides. This wide-brimmed hat is one of the Farmer's two defining features.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to hold the scythe upright. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

---

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Farmer's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The wide-brimmed hat (co-primary identifier):** A flat, wide cylinder (Warm Brown) sitting on top of the head. The brim should extend about 50% past the head on all sides — wide enough to be clearly visible from the isometric camera. Optionally, add a very short cylinder on top of the brim as the crown. This sun-shielding hat immediately says "outdoor field worker."
- **The scythe (co-primary identifier):** A long, thin cylinder handle (Tan) held upright — taller than the character. At the top, a curved flat blade (Steel Grey) sweeps to one side. The blade should be a flattened, curved wedge shape. The scythe's height makes the Farmer one of the tallest-silhouette serfs in the game.
- **No apron or body modifications:** The Farmer wears base clothing — the hat and scythe provide all the visual identity needed.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm, golden directional sunlight from above-left — evoking a harvest-time field atmosphere.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The wide-brimmed hat and tall scythe should create a distinctive silhouette from the isometric game camera angle, immediately communicating the Farmer's role.
- The scythe blade should catch the light, providing a bright steel accent against the warm brown palette.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
