# Goldsmith — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Goldsmith"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Goldsmith is a **resource processing serf** — working at the Goldsmith/Mint to produce Gold Bars from Gold Ore and Coal. This character is a precision craftsman, identified by a yellow magnifying glass and gold color accents. The Goldsmith should look meticulous, refined, and detail-oriented.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be precise, refined, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Goldsmith character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends forward, holding the magnifying glass near the face. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Goldsmith's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The magnifying glass (primary identifier):** A small sphere (Yellow `#F0C030`) representing the lens, attached to a short cylinder handle (Yellow). The lens should be approximately 25% of the body width. Position it held in one hand near the face, as if the Goldsmith is inspecting a piece of gold. The bright yellow color is unique to the Goldsmith among serfs and makes the tool highly visible.
- **Yellow chest patch (optional):** A small flat cuboid (Yellow) on the chest as a guild insignia or golden brooch — reinforcing the gold-working theme.
- **Gold bar (optional carried item):** A small bright cuboid (Gold `#FFD700`) held in the other hand or visible on the character — representing the precious output. The bright gold color provides a luxurious accent.
- **No apron or hat:** The Goldsmith is a clean, refined profession — keep the design elegant with the yellow magnifying glass as the focus.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and make the yellow magnifying glass gleam.
- A subtle warm ambient fill light to give the gold accents a rich appearance.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The bright yellow magnifying glass should catch the eye immediately from the isometric game camera angle — it is the only yellow tool in the serf roster.
- If the gold bar is included, it should provide a secondary bright accent that reinforces the Goldsmith's precious-metals role.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
