# Blacksmith — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Blacksmith"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Blacksmith is a **resource processing serf** — working at the Blacksmith/Armory to forge Swords and Shields from Iron Bars and Coal. This is a physically imposing serf — slightly bulkier than others, wielding a large hammer and wearing a very dark apron. The Blacksmith should look strong, powerful, and ready to shape metal.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be powerful, imposing, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

Explain step-by-step how to model the Blacksmith character, starting from the shared base serf model with a size modification:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), but **slightly wider** than the standard serf — approximately 10–15% wider to suggest a bulkier, more muscular build. Keep the squat, chunky proportions but make this serf visibly beefier.

- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to the side holding the large hammer. Arms may be slightly thicker than standard (Light Brown) to match the bulkier body.

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Blacksmith's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter and from the Builder:

- **The large hammer (co-primary identifier):** A cuboid head (Dark Grey `#5A5A5A`) on a cylinder handle (Tan). **Larger than the Builder's hammer** — approximately 40% of the body width for the head. This is a forging hammer, not a construction hammer. The size difference from the Builder's smaller hammer is the key distinction between these two hammer-carrying serfs.
- **The soot black apron (co-primary identifier):** A flat cuboid (Soot Black `#2A2A2A`) attached to the front of the body, extending from chest to below the waist. This is the darkest apron of any serf — nearly black from forge soot. The dark apron + large hammer + bulky body creates a distinctly different look from the Builder (dark brown apron + small hammer + normal body).
- **Bulkier build:** The slightly wider body is a subtle but important detail — the Blacksmith should feel heavier and more powerful than other serfs.
- **No hat or headwear:** The Blacksmith relies on the large hammer, dark apron, and bulky build for identification.
