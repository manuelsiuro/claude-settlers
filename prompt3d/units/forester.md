# Forester — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Forester"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Forester is a **resource gathering serf** — planting new trees to sustain the wood supply. This character works hand-in-hand with the Woodcutter, ensuring the forests regenerate. The Forester should look nature-oriented and gentle, carrying a small sapling and wearing green accents that tie them to the forest.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be earthy, nature-connected, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Body / Clothing** — Light Brown (`#C4A56E`)
- **Head / Skin** — Light Peach (`#F5D5B8`)
- **Green accent band** — Forest Green (`#4A7A3D`)
- **Sapling cone** — Forest Green (`#4A7A3D`)
- **Sapling trunk** — Tan (`#D2A86E`)

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Forester character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends forward holding the sapling. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

---

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Forester's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The sapling (primary identifier):** A small cone (Forest Green) on a tiny cylinder trunk (Tan). The cone should be about 40% of the body height — clearly visible as a miniature tree. Hold it in one extended arm, as if the Forester is about to plant it. This is the character's most important distinguishing feature.
- **Green accent band:** A thin cylinder or torus (Forest Green) wrapped around the body at chest height, representing a forester's sash or band. This provides a splash of green that immediately ties the character to their nature-oriented role.
- **Optional green chest patch:** A small flat cuboid (Forest Green) on the chest as an alternative or addition to the band — like a ranger's insignia.
- **No hat or heavy tools:** The Forester is a gentle profession — keep the design light and nature-focused.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and highlight the green sapling.
- A subtle green-tinted ambient fill to reinforce the nature theme.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The green sapling and green band should be clearly visible from the isometric game camera angle, immediately communicating the Forester's role.
- The green accents provide strong contrast against the light brown body — favor bold color separation.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
