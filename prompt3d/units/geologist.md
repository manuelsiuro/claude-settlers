# Geologist — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Geologist"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Geologist is an **exploration serf** — prospecting mountain ranges to discover ore deposits (Iron, Gold, Coal). This is an adventurous, scholarly character who scouts ahead of the settlement's expansion. The Geologist should look well-traveled and knowledgeable, wearing a wide-brimmed hat and carrying a map scroll.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be adventurous, scholarly, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Geologist character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The hat:** A wide, flat cylinder (Warm Brown) placed on top of the head — similar to the Farmer's hat but in a warmer brown tone. The brim extends about 50% past the head on all sides.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to the side holding the map scroll. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Geologist's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter and from the Farmer:

- **The wide-brimmed hat (co-primary identifier):** A flat, wide cylinder (Warm Brown `#A0724A`) sitting on top of the head. Similar shape to the Farmer's hat — both are outdoor professions that need sun protection. The Geologist is distinguished from the Farmer by carrying a map scroll instead of a scythe.
- **The map scroll (co-primary identifier):** A small rolled cylinder (Tan `#D2A86E`) held in one hand. Approximately 40% of the body height. The scroll suggests knowledge, exploration, and discovery — it is the defining item that separates the Geologist from all other hat-wearing serfs. Position it held out to the side, as if the Geologist is consulting their map.
- **No apron or heavy tools:** The Geologist is a light, mobile profession — keep the design simple with just the hat and scroll. This character needs to traverse mountain terrain quickly.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette, especially the wide hat brim.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The wide-brimmed hat and map scroll should be clearly visible from the isometric game camera angle — the hat provides a distinctive top-down silhouette while the scroll communicates the scholarly role.
- The Geologist reads as "Farmer without the scythe, with a scroll instead" — ensure the scroll is prominent enough to make this distinction clear.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
