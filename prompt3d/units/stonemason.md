# Stonemason — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Stonemason"** (Quarry Worker) character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Stonemason is a **resource gathering serf** — working at quarries to extract stone blocks. This is a strong, physical laborer who swings a pickaxe all day. The character should look tough and durable, defined by the pickaxe they carry.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be sturdy, industrial, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Pickaxe head** — Dark Grey (`#5A5A5A`)

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Stonemason character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to the side or over the shoulder holding the pickaxe. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Stonemason's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The pickaxe (primary identifier):** A cuboid head (Dark Grey) with pointed ends on a thin cylinder handle (Tan). The pickaxe head should be a horizontal bar shape — wider than the axe blade, with both ends tapering to points. Approximately 35% of the body width. Attach it to one arm, resting over the shoulder or held at the ready. This distinguishes the Stonemason from the Woodcutter (who carries an axe with a single-sided blade).
- **No special attire:** The Stonemason wears only the base light brown clothing — no apron, no hat. Like the Woodcutter, the tool alone provides the visual identity. The pickaxe's distinctive double-pointed shape is what sets this character apart.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and catch the pickaxe head.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The dark grey pickaxe should provide strong contrast against the light brown body, making the Stonemason instantly recognizable from the isometric game camera angle.
- Ensure the double-pointed pickaxe shape reads clearly — this is the key visual difference from the Woodcutter's single-bladed axe.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
