# Builder — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Builder"** character in a low-poly 3D style.

The Builder is the **construction serf** — responsible for constructing every building in the settlement. When a player places a building, a Builder carries materials to the site and hammers it into existence. The character should look sturdy and capable, with a hammer in hand and a work apron.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the character. The final look should be practical, hardworking, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Body / Clothing** — Light Brown (`#C4A56E`)
- **Head / Skin** — Light Peach (`#F5D5B8`)
- **Apron** — Dark Brown (`#5C3A1E`)
- **Hammer head** — Steel Grey (`#7A7A7A`)
- **Hammer handle** — Tan (`#D2A86E`)

---

## 2. BASE MODEL & PROPORTIONS

Explain step-by-step how to model the Builder character, starting from the shared base serf model:

- **The body:** An upright cylinder or slightly rounded cuboid (Light Brown), representing basic clothing. Keep proportions squat and chunky — approximately 1.5× as tall as wide for a charming low-poly feel.
- **The head:** A sphere (Light Peach) placed directly on top of the body. Scale it slightly larger than realistic for readability — about 60–70% of the body width.
- **The limbs (simplified):** One arm extends to the side holding the hammer. The other arm rests at the body or extends slightly. Arms are small cylinders or thin cuboids (Light Brown).

---

## 3. PROFESSION-SPECIFIC FEATURES

Describe how to model the Builder's distinguishing elements that set them apart from the base Transporter:

- **The hammer (primary identifier):** A small cuboid head (Steel Grey) on a thin cylinder handle (Tan). The hammer head is approximately 30% of the body width. Attach it to one arm, angled slightly as if ready to swing. This is the Builder's most important visual element.
- **The work apron:** A flat cuboid (Dark Brown) attached to the front of the body, extending from chest to just below the waist. Slightly narrower than the body — it should look like a protective leather apron. This is an optional but recommended detail.
- **No hat or special headwear:** The Builder is bare-headed, relying on the hammer and apron for identification.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Use warm directional sunlight from above-left to clearly define the character's silhouette and highlight the hammer.
- A subtle ambient fill light to prevent harsh shadows on the small character.
- Present on a neutral ground plane — units are not shown with environmental context.
- The hammer should be clearly visible from the isometric game camera angle, immediately communicating the Builder's role.
- The dark apron should provide good contrast against the light brown body.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
