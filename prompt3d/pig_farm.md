# Pig Farm — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Pig Farm"** in a low-poly 3D style using software like Blender or Maya.

The Pig Farm is a **resource processing building** — it raises Pigs using Grain as input. The Pigs are then sent to the Slaughterhouse to produce Meat. It should look like a rustic farm building with an enclosed pen containing cute low-poly pig shapes.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be rustic, pastoral, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Pig Farm. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The sty (barn):** A long, low cuboid (Light Brown) — stretched along one axis, like a long stable. Low height — pigs don't need much headroom. This is the main building where the Pig Farmer works.
- **The roof:** A simple sloped cuboid roof (Dark Brown). Slightly overhanging to provide some shelter over the pen entrance.
- **The pen:** An adjacent enclosed area made of thin vertical cuboid fence posts (Dark Brown) with horizontal cuboid rails connecting them. The pen should be roughly the same size as the sty building or larger — pigs need space to roam.
- **The pen ground:** The ground inside the pen should be a different color (Muddy Brown) from the surrounding grass — trampled, muddy earth.
- **The door:** A wide opening on the sty where it connects to the pen — pigs move between the two.

---

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key pig farm elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Pigs:** 2–3 tiny pinkish rounded cuboids or spheres (Pink) inside the pen. Each pig is essentially a slightly elongated sphere/cuboid for the body, 4 tiny cylinder legs, a small sphere head, and tiny triangular ears (Darker Pink). A tiny curly cylinder or cone for the tail. Keep them very simple and cute.
- **Feeding trough:** A long, low, U-shaped channel (Grey) — a cuboid with the top hollowed out — placed inside the pen. Filled with tiny yellow cubes (grain).
- **Grain sacks (input):** 2–3 burlap-colored sacks (Burlap Tan) stacked near the sty entrance — the Grain that feeds the pigs.
- **Mud puddles:** 1–2 darker flat shapes in the pen ground — pigs love mud.
- **Water bucket:** A small cylinder (grey) near the trough.
- **Hay bales:** 1–2 small cuboids (golden tan) inside or near the sty — bedding material.
- **Fence gate:** One section of the pen fence with a slightly different arrangement — a gate for the Pig Farmer to enter.
- **Surrounding grass:** Lush Meadow Green grass tufts around the outside of the pen, contrasting with the muddy interior.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Warm, pastoral sunlight — similar to the Farm. The pig farm is a cheerful, agricultural building.
- Good lighting on the pen area to make the pink pigs visible and cute.
- A soft ambient fill to keep the muddy pen readable without being dreary.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — both the sty building and the pen with pigs should be visible. The pigs are the visual highlight.
- The contrast between the green surrounding grass and the muddy pen interior immediately tells the story.
- The overall feel should be lively, pastoral, and slightly humorous — little pink pigs in a muddy pen.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
