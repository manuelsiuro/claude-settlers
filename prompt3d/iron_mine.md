# Iron Mine — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Iron Mine"** in a low-poly 3D style using software like Blender or Maya.

The Iron Mine is a **resource gathering building** — it employs a Miner who extracts Iron Ore from mountain deposits discovered by Geologists. It requires Food (Fish, Bread, or Meat) as input to keep miners fed. The building appears as a reinforced entrance built into the mountainside, with a distinctive red ore indicator cube.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be industrial, mountain-hewn, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Mine entrance structure** — Dark Grey (`#5A5A5A`)
- **Mine shaft opening** — Very Dark / Black (`#1A1A1A`)
- **Iron ore indicator** — Red (`#C0392B`)
- **Mountain rock** — Rocky Grey (`#8A8A8A`) and Dark Rock (`#6A6A6A`)
- **Support timber beams** — Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Rail track** — Dark Steel (`#4A4A4A`)
- **Ore cart** — Rusty Brown (`#8B4513`)
- **Ground/dirt** — Mountain Earth (`#7A6A5A`)
- **Iron ore chunks** — Reddish Brown (`#A04030`)

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Iron Mine. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The mountain backdrop:** A large, angular mass of cuboids (Rocky Grey, Dark Rock) stacked and rotated to suggest a mountain face. The mine is built into this — the mountain is part of the building's visual identity.
- **The mine entrance structure:** A dark grey cuboid (Dark Grey) built flush against the mountain face — a reinforced entrance frame. Wider than it is tall, like a fortified tunnel mouth.
- **The mine shaft opening:** A smaller, darker rectangle (Very Dark / Black) centered on the front face of the entrance structure — the actual tunnel going into the mountain. Make it dark and foreboding.
- **Support timber beams:** Thin cuboids (Brown) framing the shaft opening — horizontal lintel on top, vertical posts on sides. Classic mine entrance timber framing.
- **The iron ore indicator:** A small red cube (Red `#C0392B`) placed prominently near the entrance — this color-coded marker tells the player at a glance that this is an Iron Mine, not a Coal or Gold mine.

---

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key mine elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Ore cart:** A small boxy shape (Rusty Brown) on tiny cylinder wheels — a mine cart sitting on rails near the entrance, filled with small reddish-brown cubes (iron ore chunks).
- **Rail tracks:** Two thin parallel cuboids (Dark Steel) extending from the mine entrance outward a short distance.
- **Iron ore pile:** A small mound of reddish-brown irregular cubes near the entrance — extracted ore awaiting transport.
- **Pickaxe:** A T-shaped tool leaning against the entrance timbers.
- **Support beam stack:** A few horizontal brown cylinders stacked nearby — spare timber for shoring up tunnels.
- **Lantern:** A tiny cuboid with a small yellow/orange cube (warm glow) hung near the entrance — miners need light.
- **Rocky debris:** Scattered grey and reddish cubes around the entrance, rubble from excavation.
- **Sparse mountain terrain:** Very little vegetation — rocky ground, a few small boulders, maybe one scraggly bush.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Strong directional light from above to illuminate the entrance structure while the shaft opening remains dramatically dark.
- Cool grey ambient fill suggesting a mountain environment.
- A very subtle warm light inside the shaft opening (deep orange) to hint at activity within.
- The red ore indicator cube should be clearly visible and well-lit — it's the key identifier.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the mine entrance should face the camera, with the mountain rising behind it.
- The overall atmosphere should feel deep, industrial, and slightly ominous — the earth is being carved open.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
