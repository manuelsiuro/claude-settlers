# Watchtower — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Watchtower"** in a low-poly 3D style using software like Blender or Maya.

The Watchtower is a **mid-tier military building** — it expands territory more than a Guard Hut and houses 5–7 Knights. It is tall and imposing, with a crenellated top and a red flag. The tallest military structure besides the Castle itself.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be tall, vigilant, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Watchtower. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The tower body:** A tall, relatively thin cylinder or square prism (Stone Grey). This is the dominant form — make it noticeably taller than all civilian buildings. The height is the Watchtower's defining characteristic. If using a cylinder, an 8-sided prism gives a nice geometric low-poly look.
- **The top platform:** A slightly wider cylinder or square prism on top of the tower (Stone Grey). This creates the overhanging observation deck.
- **Crenellations:** Small cubes arranged around the upper edge of the top platform — classic castle battlements. Leave gaps (merlons and crenels) for a medieval fortified look.
- **The flag:** A small red pyramid (Red) on the very top — the military identifier. Alternatively, a thin pole (cylinder) with a flat red plane as a pennant.
- **The door:** A small arched or rectangular opening (Dark Brown) at the base of the tower.
- **Arrow slits:** Multiple thin, tall rectangular indentations at various heights spiraling up the tower — narrow defensive windows.
- **The foundation:** A wider, squat cuboid or cylinder (Dark Grey) at the base — a solid stone platform.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key watchtower elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **A ladder or external stair:** Thin cuboid rungs arranged diagonally against the tower exterior — access to the top platform. This visual detail emphasizes the height.
- **Torch sconces:** 2–3 tiny torches at various heights on the tower exterior — the tower is lit at night for surveillance.
- **A banner/tapestry:** A small, flat rectangular plane (Deep Red) hanging from the top platform — a faction flag hanging down the side of the tower.
- **Weapon rack at base:** A small wooden frame with swords and shields near the entrance.
- **A lookout on top (optional):** Tiny suggestion of a guard figure or a scope/telescope prop on the top platform.
- **Defensive stakes:** Small pointed wooden stakes (thin inverted cones) around the base — anti-cavalry defenses.
- **Stacked stone supplies:** A few grey cubes near the base — maintenance supplies for the stone structure.
- **Cleared ground:** The area around the watchtower is bare dirt/gravel (Dirt Brown) — clear sightlines in all directions.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Strong directional light from the side to emphasize the tower's height through long vertical shadows.
- The crenellations should cast interesting shadow patterns on the top platform.
- The red flag/pyramid at the top should be the most colorful element, standing out against the stone grey tower.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the full height of the tower should be visible, making it clearly the tallest non-Castle structure.
- A slight cool ambient fill to enhance the stone fortress feeling.
- The overall atmosphere should feel commanding and watchful — a sentinel overlooking the kingdom's borders.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
