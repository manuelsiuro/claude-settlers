# Guard Hut — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Guard Hut"** in a low-poly 3D style using software like Blender or Maya.

The Guard Hut is the **basic military outpost** — it expands the player's territory and houses 2–3 Knights. It requires Swords and Shields to recruit Knights. Smallest military building — compact, robust, and fortified with a distinctive red banner/roof cap.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be compact, fortified, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Guard Hut. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The main structure:** A small, robust-looking square cuboid (Dark Grey). Thick walls, compact footprint — this is a fortified military post, not a home. Slightly squatter than civilian buildings of similar size.
- **The roof/banner:** A slightly smaller, flat red cuboid on top of the structure, or alternatively a small red pyramid (Red) — the red military identifier that marks all military buildings. This red cap is visible from a distance and signals "military" on the map.
- **The door:** A dark rectangular indentation (Very Dark Grey) — a heavy, reinforced entrance. Narrower than civilian doors.
- **The foundation:** A slightly wider low cuboid (Medium Grey) — a stone base making the structure look sturdier.
- **Arrow slits:** 1–2 very thin, tall rectangular indentations on the walls — narrow windows for defense.

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key guard hut elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **A weapon rack:** A small wooden frame (Brown cuboids) near the entrance holding 1–2 swords (thin elongated cuboids, Bright Steel) and a shield (small circular disc, Red on Grey).
- **A shield emblem:** A small circular or octagonal shape (Red on Grey) mounted on the wall above the door — the military insignia.
- **Torch sconces:** 1–2 tiny cylinders with small orange cubes (Warm Orange flame) on the wall flanking the entrance — military buildings are lit for vigilance.
- **A flagpole:** A thin vertical cylinder rising from the red roof cap with a small red pennant (flat plane) — the faction flag.
- **Patrol path markers:** A few thin, short wooden posts (Brown) in the ground around the hut, suggesting a patrol route.
- **Sandbags or stone barriers:** Small cuboid shapes stacked in front of the hut, suggesting a defensive position.
- **Sparse, trampled ground:** The area around the guard hut is dirt and gravel (Dirt Brown), not lush grass — this is a military zone.
- **A training dummy (optional):** A T-shaped wooden figure nearby for knight training.

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Clear, strong directional light emphasizing the solid, blocky structure.
- The red roof/banner should be the most prominent color — it's the military identifier.
- Contrast between the dark grey walls and red accents should be sharp and clear.
- Torch light (warm orange) near the entrance adds a touch of warmth to the otherwise stern structure.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the guard hut should look compact and solid, clearly a fortified outpost.
- The overall atmosphere should feel vigilant and defensive — the first line of territorial expansion.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
