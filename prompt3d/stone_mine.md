# Stone Mine — 3D Modeling Prompt

Act as an expert 3D artist and instructor specializing in low-poly modeling and stylized game art. Your task is to provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on how to model a stylized **"Stone Mine"** in a low-poly 3D style.

The Stone Mine is a **resource gathering building** — it employs a Miner who extracts Stone from deep mountain deposits (as opposed to the surface Quarry). It is differentiated from the Quarry by being a true mine shaft built into the mountainside. Its ore indicator is a light grey cube.

Please provide a structured, detailed explanation covering the geometry, specific shapes, and color palettes for each part of the scene. The final look should be solid, utilitarian, mountain-hewn, and characteristic of a classic fantasy low-poly aesthetic (flat shading, distinct geometric shapes, no complex textures).

---

## 1. THE COLOR PALETTE

Provide a specific low-poly color palette with exact HEX codes for:

- **Mine entrance structure** — Dark Grey (`#5A5A5A`)
- **Mine shaft opening** — Very Dark (`#1A1A1A`)
- **Stone ore indicator** — Light Grey (`#B8B8B8`)
- **Mountain rock** — Rocky Grey (`#8A8A8A`) and Medium Grey (`#7A7A7A`)
- **Support timber beams** — Brown (`#8B5E3C`)
- **Cut stone blocks** — Light Grey (`#B0B0B0`) with Warm Grey accents (`#A0A098`)
- **Ground/dirt** — Stony Earth (`#8A7A6A`)
- **Rail track** — Dark Steel (`#4A4A4A`)
- **Ore cart** — Weathered Wood (`#6A5030`)

## 2. CORE STRUCTURE & GEOMETRY

Explain step-by-step how to model the Stone Mine. Include descriptions of the shapes and primitive meshes to use for:

- **The mountain backdrop:** A large angular mass of cuboids (Rocky Grey, Medium Grey) — the stone mine's mountain should look particularly solid and blocky, suggesting high-quality stone deposits.
- **The mine entrance structure:** A dark grey cuboid (Dark Grey) built into the mountain — the reinforced entrance. Similar to other mines but slightly wider, as stone blocks are bulkier to transport.
- **The mine shaft opening:** A wide, dark rectangle on the front face — the tunnel entrance. Slightly wider and squarer than ore mine openings.
- **Support timber beams:** Thick cuboids (Brown) framing the shaft — stone mines need extra support due to the size of extracted blocks.
- **The stone ore indicator:** A small light grey cube (Light Grey `#B8B8B8`) placed near the entrance. The pale, neutral cube distinguishes this mine from Iron (red), Coal (black), and Gold (yellow).

---

## 3. PROPS AND ENVIRONMENT DETAILS

Describe how to model the key stone mine elements to tell a story. Break down the modeling steps for:

- **Cut stone blocks:** Several clean-edged cubes and cuboids (Light Grey, Warm Grey) neatly arranged near the entrance — unlike rough ore chunks, these are somewhat uniform, cut stone ready for construction.
- **Ore cart:** A wide, flat cart (Weathered Wood) on rail tracks — designed for heavy stone blocks rather than loose ore.
- **Rail tracks:** Two sturdy parallel cuboids extending from the entrance.
- **Chisels and hammers:** Small tool props (Steel Grey heads, Brown handles) near the stone blocks.
- **Stone dust:** Flat, pale grey shapes scattered around the ground near the work area.
- **Stacked stones:** A neatly stacked arrangement of cuboids suggesting organized stone storage — masons are methodical workers.
- **Lantern:** Warm-glowing lantern near the entrance.
- **Heavy rope or chain:** A small coiled cylinder near the entrance for hauling heavy blocks.
- **Rocky ground:** The terrain around the Stone Mine should be naturally rocky — the building blends with its environment.

---

## 4. LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION

Briefly suggest a lighting setup to make the flat-shaded low-poly colors pop:

- Clean, neutral directional sunlight — the stone mine's palette is entirely greys, so lighting must create clear value distinctions.
- A cool ambient fill to keep the greys from blending together.
- Use shadow to distinguish between the mountain backdrop, the entrance structure, and the cut stone blocks — three similar colors that need separation.
- The light grey ore indicator should be visible despite being grey-on-grey — position it in direct light.
- Present from an isometric orthographic camera angle — the neatly arranged stone blocks should be prominently visible.
- The overall atmosphere should feel solid and dependable — stone is the literal building block of civilization.

Make your instructions easy to follow, highly descriptive regarding the 3D shapes (e.g., "extrude," "bevel," "scale down the top face," "icosphere"), and inspiring!
